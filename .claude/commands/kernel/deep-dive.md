# /deep-dive

Pressure-test a chosen business into a go/no-go + a real first-90-days plan, on evidence. Loop B of the venture-loop family (runs after `/competition`).

## Usage

```
/deep-dive <business>
```

| Argument | Purpose | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| `<business>` | The chosen business to validate | `/deep-dive local AI receptionist for mobile mechanics` |

## What It Does

Names the riskiest assumptions, sizes the market (TAM/SAM/SOM), sharpens the customer, gathers real demand evidence (the cheapest live test), pulls competition from `/competition`, checks the economics, the build/ops + human-in-the-loop line, and stress-tests the risks — then a **go / no-go + a concrete first-90-days plan** (or a clean kill with reasons). Output is the **quickest view to the pertinent info**, never a long doc. Every run is saved.
