# /trends

Idea-hunter — find rising trends / tech / regulatory catalysts in a space and the business ideas they newly enable. Part of the `/source` family.

## Usage

```
/trends <seed theme or space>
```

## What It Does

Takes a seed (e.g. "local services", "AI automation") and hunts the **why-now** catalysts — new tools, platform shifts, regulation, cost curves — then extracts the business ideas each newly makes possible. Dedups against the assay ledger, ranks by signal, feeds `/assay`. Lean output, saved.
