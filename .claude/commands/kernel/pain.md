# /pain

Idea-hunter — mine real pain in a space (what people complain about, hack around, pay badly to solve). Part of the `/source` family.

## Usage

```
/pain <seed theme or space>
```

## What It Does

Takes a seed and mines genuine demand signals — complaints, workarounds, "I'd pay for X", bad incumbent solutions (Reddit, reviews, forums, communities) — then extracts business ideas that solve them. Real pain = real demand. Dedups against the assay ledger, ranks, feeds `/assay`. Lean output, saved.
