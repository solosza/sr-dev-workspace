# /bookmarks

Idea-hunter — mine your own saved reels / links / notes for business ideas. Part of the `/source` family.

## Usage

```
/bookmarks [seed theme or space]
```

## What It Does

Mines the operator's own saved signal — bookmarked reels, links, notes (and the X bookmark scanner) — for business ideas, optionally filtered to a seed. Your saves are a curated pain/interest signal you already trust. Dedups against the assay ledger, ranks, feeds `/assay`. Lean output, saved.
