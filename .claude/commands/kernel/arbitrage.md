# /arbitrage

Idea-hunter — find what works in one market/geo/vertical that's missing in another (proven-elsewhere transplant). Part of the `/source` family.

## Usage

```
/arbitrage <seed theme or space>
```

## What It Does

Takes a seed and finds proven models, tools, or offers thriving in market/geo/vertical A that are absent or weak in B — the transplant opportunity. Lower risk (already proven somewhere), clear "why here, why now". Dedups against the assay ledger, ranks, feeds `/assay`. Lean output, saved.
