# /assets

Idea-hunter — reverse from YOUR unique assets to ideas only you can build/own. Part of the `/source` family.

## Usage

```
/assets <seed theme or space>
```

## What It Does

Takes a seed and works backwards from the operator's unfair advantages — the kernel/governance, owned data, skills, channels, prior builds — to business ideas that *fit-to-you* and are hard for others to copy. The highest-conviction source (built-in moat). Dedups against the assay ledger, ranks, feeds `/assay`. Lean output, saved.
