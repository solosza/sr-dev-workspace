# /productize

Expansion angle — turn a service into a product/SaaS to escape hours-for-dollars.

## Usage

```
/productize <business or idea>
```

## What It Does

Asks: what repeatable part of this service becomes a product (SaaS, template, tool) that scales without your time? The path from labor to leverage. Think-bigger, generative. Output = quickest view. Saved (report + ledger + venture record).
