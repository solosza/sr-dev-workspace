### Modern Sets — The Losers

Most modern sets have **negative ROI (-10% to -20%)** when opened. Even sealed, many modern sets depreciate:
- Overprinted sets lose value within 12-18 months
- Sets without chase cards or competitive relevance fade fastest
- Only ~20-30% of modern sets appreciate significantly over 3+ years
- Modern singles have corrected **20-45%** from 2025 peaks
- Modern sealed product down **20-50%** from peak

## Hidden Cost Breakdown

### Grading Costs (PSA, 2026)

| Tier | Per Card Fee | Turnaround | Realistic All-In Cost |
|------|-------------|------------|----------------------|
| Value | $25 | 45-65 business days | $45-$60/card |
| Regular | $50 | 20-30 business days | $70-$85/card |
| Express | $100 | 5-10 business days | $120-$135/card |
| Super Express | $200 | 1-3 business days | $220-$235/card |

**Hidden fees beyond base price:**
- PSA membership: $99/year (required)
- Outbound shipping: $10-$28
- Return shipping: $10-$20
- Packing supplies: $5-$15
- Shipping insurance: 1-3% of declared value
- CGC is cheaper all-in for <20 cards/year, but PSA commands higher resale premiums

## Print Run & Saturation Risk (CRITICAL)

**The Pokemon Company printed 10.2 BILLION cards between March 2024 and March 2025.** That's 18.3% of all Pokemon cards ever produced in a single year.

### Market Bifurcation
- **Vintage (pre-2003)**: Insulated from overprinting. Fixed, shrinking supply. Up 30-50% into 2026.
- **Modern (2020+)**: Directly impacted. Overprinting → excess supply → price corrections of 20-50% from peaks.

### Flipper-Driven Market
- Over 80% of recent modern card sales are driven by "flippers" chasing quick profits
- When speculation is the primary driver, corrections are inevitable
- The market is "not collapsing" but experiencing a "healthy correction" — depends who you ask

### What Survives
- Sets with genuinely low print runs
- Sets with iconic chase cards (alt arts, secret rares)
- Sets tied to competitive play relevance
- Anything vintage (supply only decreases)
- Japanese product (lower print runs than English)
