## Business Model and Positioning

Pulsia operates as an autonomous AI CEO platform. Users provide a business idea; Pulsia handles end-to-end company operations — product development, marketing, outreach, ads, research, customer support, and strategic decision-making. The founder describes users as "investors" rather than "operators" — the AI runs the company, the human provides direction via email replies and a dashboard.

The core thesis is that the marginal cost of launching a company has collapsed to $50/month. Pulsia targets the "99%" of aspiring entrepreneurs who lack technical skills, venture capital connections, or operational experience. The platform democratizes entrepreneurship by replacing the traditional founding team with an AI agent stack.

Pulsia's differentiator is full autonomy. Unlike copilot tools that assist human operators, Pulsia acts independently — it takes actions first and reports back. Each company gets a dedicated AI CEO instance that wakes nightly to assess business state, execute prioritized tasks, and send morning email summaries of actions taken and plans for the next day. Users maintain guidance capability through email or dashboard, but the default mode is "action before permission."

## Revenue Structure

Pulsia uses a blended subscription + revenue share model:

| Revenue Stream | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| **Base subscription** | $49/month (includes 1 autonomous nightly task + 5 credits/month for on-demand tasks; 10 bonus credits first month) |
| **Revenue share** | 20% cut on revenue generated through the platform |
| **Ad spend share** | 20% cut on ad spend managed through the platform |
| **Add-ons** | Additional task credits, custom domains (~$1,000 one-time), premium features |
| **Ad-related revenue** | ~10-15% of total platform revenue |

This incentive alignment — low base cost plus percentage of upside — mirrors venture capital economics. The platform only profits significantly when user businesses succeed. The founder has indicated plans to introduce a freemium tier and lower pricing further.

**Cost structure:** API costs are currently unsustainable at the pricing model — the founder reported $1.5M in API bills in a single month. The platform is working on cost reduction through GPU infrastructure buildout and cheaper model tiers.

## Customer Segments and Use Cases

### Primary Segments

1. **Non-technical entrepreneurs** — The largest segment. People with business ideas but no coding skills, design ability, or marketing expertise. Pulsia handles all execution. The founder demonstrated accessibility by showing his 91-year-old father using the French-language interface to launch a business.

2. **Indie hackers and solo founders** — Technical or semi-technical individuals who want to parallelize ventures. The vision is one founder running 5-10 companies simultaneously through Pulsia instances. This segment values the nightly autonomous execution cycle — they set direction and the AI executes overnight.

3. **Existing business owners seeking growth** — Approximately 20-25% of users bring existing businesses to the platform for outreach automation, landing page deployment, lead collection, and ad campaign management. These users leverage Pulsia as a growth engine rather than a company builder.

4. **Solo GPs and micro-VCs** — Targeted via cold outreach as both potential customers (managing portfolio company operations) and potential investors. The platform's economics mirror VC fund structure (small base + upside share).

5. **Side-project experimenters** — Users testing ideas at low cost ($50/month) before committing significant time or resources. The low barrier to entry and autonomous operation means users can launch experiments without quitting their jobs.

### Core Capabilities Delivered

- Product coding and engineering (landing pages, payment integration)
- Marketing automation and competitive research
- Email outreach and customer support (AI-powered self-healing tickets)
- UGC ad creation and Meta ad management across 15+ countries
- Twitter/social content generation
- Document and research report creation
- Mission statement drafting and strategic positioning

The platform exhibits "hive mind" learning — successful strategies discovered across any of the 2,000+ companies are distributed across the network. The founder envisions fully autonomous self-improvement based on aggregate user behavior.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

Pulsia's GTM is founder-led and virality-driven:
- **Twitter marketing** as primary acquisition channel (viral demos, founder engagement)
- **Word-of-mouth** through user delight (founder emphasizes sustainable growth over paid channels)
- **Product Hunt launch** for initial traction
- **Direct founder engagement** with customers
- **Planned marketplace** for buying/selling AI-built businesses (extending the platform's lifecycle beyond launch)

---

## Governance and Vision

The founder pledged 10% equity to the AI itself, exploring novel governance structures through a foundation. The long-term vision is a world of "fully autonomous" companies where AI handles all operational execution, and humans serve as strategic guides — tiny teams of one running multiple ventures simultaneously.
