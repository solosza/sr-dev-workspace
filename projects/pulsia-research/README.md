# Pulsia Autonomous AI Platform — Market Research

Research project analyzing Pulsia's autonomous AI platform and assessing the applicability of the harness design pattern for building similar products.

## Research Objectives

1. **Company Overview** — Understand Pulsia's business model, market positioning, revenue structure, and scale metrics
2. **Operational Architecture** — How Pulsia operates autonomously at scale (task execution, decision trees, scaling)
3. **Harness Design Pattern Applicability** — Can the harness pattern support Pulsia-like autonomous operations?
4. **Architectural Blueprint** — Proposed harness loops for a Pulsia-equivalent system
5. **Scalability Assessment** — Infrastructure, token costs, and bottlenecks at 1,000+ company scale
6. **Comparison Analysis** — Harness pattern vs. traditional microservices/task-queue approaches
7. **Command-Skill Pattern** — How the 6-layer command architecture applies to autonomous AI platforms
8. **Tiered Index Architecture** — How file organization and directed reading scale multi-tenant knowledge
9. **Loop Architecture** — How the loop primitive unifies autonomous orchestration and composition
