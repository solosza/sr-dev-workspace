## The @WritesToProfit Thesis — Final Verdict

**Partially valid.** The core advice — "form an LLC now and use AI to build income streams" — is correct. But the specific mechanics matter:

- **Valid:** Form LLC immediately (we should). Use AI to deliver services faster (we already do). Multiple revenue streams under one entity (works until healthcare liability forces separation).
- **Invalid:** "Just subcontract govcon" (FAR 50% rule constrains this). AI content farms and prompt engineering are not sustainable business models.
- **Our edge:** We don't just "use AI" — we build AI agent infrastructure. The kernel, the platforms, and the factory are defensible products, not AI-wrapper businesses.
