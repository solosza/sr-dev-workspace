# LLC Formation Options — Hawaii vs Wyoming vs Delaware

## Summary

Comparing three states for forming an LLC to operate AI-based service businesses (agent licensing, QA consulting, government contracting, RT automation). Given Hawaii residency, the question is whether to form locally or in a business-friendly state.

## Comparison

| Factor | Hawaii | Wyoming | Delaware |
|--------|--------|---------|----------|
| **Filing fee** | $51 | $100 | $90 |
| **Annual report** | $15/year | $60/year | $300/year (franchise tax) |
| **State income tax** | 1.4%-11% (progressive) | None | 2.2%-6.6% |
| **General Excise / Sales Tax** | 4-4.5% GET on gross revenue | None | None |
| **Privacy** | Members listed publicly | Strong privacy — no member disclosure | Moderate — moving toward transparency |
| **Processing time** | 10-15 business days | 1-2 business days | 1-3 business days |
| **Registered agent** | Required (HI address) | Required (WY address, ~$100/yr) | Required (DE address, ~$100/yr) |
| **Court system** | Standard | Standard | Court of Chancery (specialized) |

## Hawaii General Excise Tax — Critical Factor

Hawaii's GET is not a sales tax. It applies to **gross revenue** (before expenses), not net income. For AI businesses:

- **4% base rate** (4.5% with county surcharge on all islands)
- Applies to **all business activities**: SaaS, consulting, services, B2B transactions
- SaaS and software (prewritten and custom) are taxable under GET
- Can pass through to customers at up to 4.712% (tax-on-tax adjustment)
- Economic nexus threshold: $100K gross income or 200+ transactions

**Impact:** On $200K gross revenue, GET costs $9,000-$9,400 before you calculate any other taxes. This is a significant drag on margin for a bootstrapped business.

## Analysis

### Hawaii LLC
- **Pros:** Cheapest to form ($51), lowest annual fees ($15), no need for out-of-state registered agent, simplest compliance
- **Cons:** GET on gross revenue is punishing for service businesses, high state income tax (up to 11%), no privacy protections
- **Best for:** If you want simplicity and plan to stay small

### Wyoming LLC
- **Pros:** No state income tax, no franchise tax, strong privacy, fast processing, modern regulations for digital businesses, low annual cost ($60)
- **Cons:** Need a registered agent in WY (~$100/yr), operating in HI still triggers HI GET on HI-sourced revenue
- **Best for:** Bootstrapped tech businesses, privacy-conscious founders, multiple revenue streams

### Delaware LLC
- **Pros:** Court of Chancery (specialized corporate law), investor credibility, 60%+ of Fortune 500 incorporated here
- **Cons:** $300/yr franchise tax, state income tax, higher compliance costs, overkill for early-stage solopreneur
- **Best for:** If raising venture capital or planning IPO

## Recommendation

**Form in Wyoming.** Rationale:

1. **No state income tax** — saves thousands annually as revenue grows
2. **Strong privacy** — founder details not publicly disclosed
3. **Low annual costs** — $60/yr vs Delaware's $300/yr
4. **Modern digital economy regulations** — designed for tech businesses
5. **SAM.gov compatible** — can register for government contracting from any state
6. **Hawaii GET still applies** to Hawaii-sourced revenue regardless of where the LLC is formed, but revenue from out-of-state clients avoids GET entirely

**Important caveat:** Forming in Wyoming does NOT avoid Hawaii GET on revenue earned from Hawaii customers/clients. But for a business with national/remote clients (govcon, SaaS licensing, remote QA consulting), most revenue will be out-of-state.

## Formation Steps (Wyoming)

1. Choose LLC name — verify availability at Wyoming Secretary of State
2. Hire Wyoming registered agent (~$100/yr)
3. File Articles of Organization online ($100, 1-2 days)
4. Get EIN from IRS (free, same day online)
5. Draft operating agreement (single-member, simple)
6. Open business bank account (wait 14 days after EIN for SAM.gov)
7. Register for Hawaii GET if any Hawaii-sourced revenue
