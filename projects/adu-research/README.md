# ADU Research

**Scope:** Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) rules for two owner-held-property jurisdictions:
- Las Vegas / Clark County, NV (City of Las Vegas, City of Henderson, City of North Las Vegas, and unincorporated Clark County each have distinct zoning code — jurisdiction is identified per-property)
- City & County of Honolulu, HI

**Created:** 2026-07-22

## Contents
- `las-vegas-clark.md` — governing-body identification, casita/ADU ordinance research, zoning/permit/process breakdown, "add an ADU" checklist
- `honolulu.md` — City & County of Honolulu ADU rules (Bill 20 / ROH Ch. 21 lineage), zoning/permit/process breakdown, "add an ADU" checklist
- `summary.md` — cross-county comparison table + per-county checklists + parcel-dependent gaps

## Caveats
Laws change. Every finding in this research is cited to a specific code section, ordinance/bill number, or official planning-department source, with a date, so it can be re-verified. This is jurisdiction-level research, not property-specific legal advice — parcel-dependent items (zone, lot size, existing coverage) are flagged for the owner to confirm against their specific parcels.
