## 2. Checklist A — Adding an ADU/casita in the Las Vegas valley (any of the four jurisdictions)

1. **Eligibility check**
   - Confirm which governing body the parcel sits in (City of Las Vegas / Henderson / North Las Vegas / unincorporated Clark County) — city/county boundaries are not intuitive from a mailing address alone. Use the county GIS/parcel lookup or call the relevant planning counter with the APN.
   - Confirm the parcel's zoning district and whether it's on the jurisdiction's single-family/residential-use table.
   - In unincorporated Clark County, confirm lot size against the 4,000 sq ft SUP threshold.
   - Confirm whether the intended structure is a full-kitchen ADU (AB 396 term) or a no-kitchen "casita" (Clark County's own narrower term) — the applicable rules differ.
2. **Design**
   - Pull current dimensional standards (max size, setbacks, height, coverage) directly from the jurisdiction's code or planning counter — several figures in the source research are flagged pending-codification (Las Vegas Ord. 6963) or unconfirmed (Clark County Ch. 30.44 PDF extraction failed; North Las Vegas Municode access blocked).
   - Confirm HOA architectural-review requirements separately from zoning — HOAs retain design authority even where zoning is now permissive.
   - Decide attached vs. detached vs. garage conversion; confirm parking (max 1 space) and utility/sewer connection approach.
3. **Permit submittal**
   - Submit building-permit application (ministerial path if conforming, per jurisdiction's post-AB-396 status above; SUP/discretionary path if the Clark County <4,000 sq ft or mobile/tiny-home-park trigger applies).
   - Budget realistic review windows: Clark County ~4–10 weeks plan review; Henderson ~4–8 weeks plan review within a longer ~12–18 month total project timeline.
4. **Build**
   - Construction per approved plans; utility/sewer trenching if required (Clark County sewer lateral $8,000–$20,000 is a cited planning figure, not a quote).
   - Standard inspections through the permit process (framing, electrical, plumbing per jurisdiction).
5. **Final inspection / Certificate of Occupancy**
   - Obtain final inspection sign-off and Certificate of Occupancy/completion before renting or occupying the unit.
   - If renting: confirm STR vs. long-term rules apply (30+ day minimum in Las Vegas/Clark County; separate STR ordinance in Henderson) before advertising the unit.

## 3. Checklist B — Adding an ADU in Honolulu

1. **Eligibility check**
   - Confirm zoning district (R-3.5/R-5/R-7.5/R-10/R-20, or Country District AG-1/AG-2).
   - Confirm the parcel is not on a Condominium Property Regime (CPR) — CPR lots are categorically excluded from ADUs.
   - Confirm whether the property already has an ʻohana unit or two principal dwellings — Ordinance 25-2 (2025) opened ADU eligibility for these previously-blocked scenarios.
   - Confirm current bedroom count on the property against the 5-bedroom-per-wastewater-system cap (see §4 gaps below) — this can block an ADU independent of zoning.
2. **Design**
   - Confirm current size tier (500 sq ft if lot <5,000 sq ft; 1,000 sq ft if ≥5,000 sq ft) and that setback/height/coverage figures cited in the source research (5 ft setback, 25 ft height) are indicative only — verify against DPP/primary ROH text for the applicable zone.
   - Confirm parking: 1 stall required unless the property qualifies for the transit-proximity waiver (within 800 ft of a bus stop or 0.5 mi of a rail station) and hasn't already used a prior ʻohana-unit parking adjustment.
3. **Permit submittal — Pre-Form gate is mandatory and precedes the building permit**
   - Submit the ʻOhana/ADU Pre-Form to DPP and obtain sign-off from: Board of Water Supply (capacity), Dept. of Environmental Services / State DOH (sewer/wastewater or onsite-system approval), Dept. of Transportation Services, and Honolulu Fire Department.
   - Only after Pre-Form sign-off, submit the standard DPP building-permit application (ministerial review under HRS §46-4.8 if the design conforms to zoning/dimensional standards — no discretionary hearing).
   - Record the owner-occupancy covenant if DPP still requires it (status post-Ordinance-25-2 is unconfirmed in the source research — ask DPP directly).
4. **Build**
   - Construction with standard inspections (framing, electrical, plumbing, final) — budget 3–12+ months total depending on DPP backlog and infrastructure sign-off complexity.
5. **Final inspection / Certificate of Occupancy**
   - Obtain final sign-off/certificate of occupancy.
   - Before renting: note the ADU-specific 6-month minimum rental term (STR/nightly rental of an ADU is prohibited), separate from the citywide 90-day minimum that applies to other residential rentals.

## 4. What you must confirm per property (parcel-dependent — this report cannot answer these)

These items determine the *actual* answer for a specific address and are outside the scope of jurisdiction-level research:

1. **Exact zoning district** — via county/city GIS parcel lookup or a direct call to the planning counter with the APN. Dimensional standards (size cap, setback, height, coverage/FAR) are set per zoning district, not uniformly per jurisdiction.
2. **Exact lot size** — determines which size tier applies (e.g., Honolulu's 500 vs. 1,000 sq ft split at 5,000 sq ft; Clark County's 4,000 sq ft SUP/ministerial split).
3. **Existing coverage / existing structures on the lot** — how much of the lot's buildable envelope the primary dwelling (and any existing ʻohana unit, guest quarters, or second principal dwelling) already consumes; also whether the lot is CPR-designated (Honolulu) or in a mobile/tiny-home park (Clark County).
4. **Sewer/water capacity** — in Honolulu this is a hard procedural gate (Board of Water Supply + DOH/DES sign-off via the Pre-Form, plus the 5-bedroom-per-wastewater-system cap); in Clark County/valley jurisdictions it is a cost driver (sewer lateral trenching) rather than a formal pre-approval gate, but must still be confirmed with the utility provider before design.
5. **HOA CC&Rs** — applies across all Las Vegas-valley jurisdictions regardless of zoning permissiveness; not researched per-HOA in this pass (named examples with active review: Summerlin, MacDonald Highlands, Anthem Country Club, Queensridge, Red Rock Country Club, Seven Hills).
6. **Current owner-occupancy requirement** — flagged as unconfirmed post-amendment in Las Vegas, Clark County, and Henderson, and mechanism-unconfirmed in Honolulu; must be verified directly with the applicable planning/DPP office at the time of application, since these figures were mid-change as of the research date.
