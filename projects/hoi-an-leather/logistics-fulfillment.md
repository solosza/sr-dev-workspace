# Logistics & Import Duties: Vietnam to US Fulfillment

## HTS Duty Rates — Chapter 42 Leather Goods

All rates are MFN (Most Favored Nation) applied to Vietnam. No preferential trade agreement reduces these.

| HTS Code | Product | Duty Rate | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-----------|-------|
| **4202.21** | Handbags (with/without shoulder strap), leather outer surface | **8-9%** ad valorem | Primary classification for crossbody bags, totes, clutches |
| **4202.22** | Handbags, textile outer surface (leather trim) | **8.4-17.6%** | Only if outer surface is fabric; avoid this classification |
| **4202.12** | Trunks, suitcases, travel bags, duffels — leather outer | **8%** ad valorem | Duffel bags, weekender bags fall here |
| **4202.31** | Wallets, card cases — leather outer | **8%** ad valorem | Small leather goods |

**Additional fees on every import:**
- Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF): 0.3464% of declared value (min $31.67, max $614.35)
- Harbor Maintenance Fee: 0.125% (sea freight only)
- No Section 301 tariffs on Vietnam-origin goods (Section 301 targets China)

**Effective landed duty cost:** ~8.5-9.5% of declared value for leather bags via air freight.

## Sea Freight Viability

| Factor | Threshold |
|--------|-----------|
| **Break-even vs. air** | ~50+ units or 100+ kg |
| **Minimum LCL shipment** | ~1 CBM (cubic meter) — roughly 100-200 bags |
| **LCL cost (Da Nang → US West Coast)** | $200-400 per CBM |
| **Transit time** | 25-35 days (+ 5-7 days port clearance) |
| **Total door-to-door** | 35-45 days |

**Verdict:** Sea freight is NOT viable for Phase 1 or Phase 2 volumes. At 25-50 units, air freight is cheaper when you factor in the per-CBM minimums, customs broker fees ($150-250 per entry), and the 4-6 week cash flow delay. Sea freight becomes rational at 100+ units per shipment.

## De Minimis Rule ($800 Threshold) — 2026 Update

**The $800 de minimis exemption has been suspended.**

- Effective August 29, 2025: US suspended duty-free treatment for low-value shipments from ALL countries
- February 2026: White House executive order continued the suspension
- Congress passed the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" — permanently repeals Section 321 effective July 1, 2027
- Civil penalties up to $10,000 for businesses that intentionally split orders to evade duties

**What this means for us:**
- Every import shipment requires a customs entry and duty payment, regardless of value
- Shipment splitting (sending multiple sub-$800 packages) is explicitly prohibited and monitored by CBP
- All shipments need a customs broker or self-filing via ACE (Automated Commercial Environment)
- Budget 8.5-9.5% duty on every import, no exceptions

**Action item:** Use DHL/FedEx for initial shipments — they handle customs brokerage as part of their service (included in shipping cost for express shipments). No need for a separate customs broker until sea freight volumes.

### 3PL Recommendation

**Phase 1 (0-30 orders/month):** Self-fulfill from home. At <30 orders, the $250-275/month 3PL minimum is a tax on low volume. Self-fulfillment costs $10-19/order all-in (shipping + packaging) vs. $15-25/order via 3PL (fees + shipping). You also maintain full QC and can include handwritten notes, custom packaging — brand-building touches that 3PLs can't do.

**Phase 2 (30-100 orders/month):** Evaluate ShipMonk. When you're consistently hitting 30+ orders/month, the time savings justify the $250/month minimum. ShipMonk's lower storage costs and no-volume-minimum approach fit a growing leather goods brand. At 50 orders/month, 3PL cost is ~$5/order for the minimum + per-order fees — reasonable.

**Phase 3 (100+ orders/month):** Move to ShipBob. Multi-warehouse distribution, 2-day shipping coverage, and strong Shopify integration make ShipBob the scaling play.

## Key Decisions & Risks

| Decision | Recommendation | Risk |
|----------|---------------|------|
| **Customs broker** | Use DHL/FedEx built-in brokerage for Phase 1-2 | At sea freight volumes, hire a licensed customs broker ($150-250/entry) |
| **Insurance** | Always insure international shipments | DHL includes $100 coverage; buy supplemental for high-value shipments |
| **Country of origin marking** | Every bag must be marked "Made in Vietnam" | CBP requires permanent country-of-origin marking on all imports |
| **Commercial invoice** | Workshop provides per shipment | Must include: description, HTS code, quantity, unit value, total value, country of origin |
| **Record keeping** | Keep all invoices, shipping docs, duty receipts for 5 years | CBP can audit imports up to 5 years after entry |
