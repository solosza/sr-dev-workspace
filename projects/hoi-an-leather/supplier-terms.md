## Payment Methods

### Recommended for Vietnamese Family Workshops

| Method | Best For | Fees | Notes |
|--------|----------|------|-------|
| **Wise (TransferWise)** | All batch sizes | ~1-2% + mid-market rate | Best overall option. Low fees, fast (1-2 business days), workshop receives VND directly to their Vietnamese bank account. Most small suppliers prefer this. |
| **PayPal** | Samples and small orders (<$500) | ~4-5% total (sender + receiver fees) | Convenient but expensive at scale. Workshop needs a PayPal account (available in Vietnam but not universal). Good for first sample order. |
| **Bank Wire (T/T)** | Large orders ($1,000+) | $25-45 flat fee per transfer | Traditional method. Workshop provides bank details (bank name, SWIFT/BIC, account number, beneficiary name). Slower (3-5 days). Best for large batches where flat fee is proportionally small. |
| **Western Union** | If workshop has no bank account | ~$5-15 per transfer | Cash pickup available throughout Vietnam. Some family workshops prefer cash. Higher effort but accessible. |

**Avoid:** Cryptocurrency (too volatile, unfamiliar to most workshops), Letters of Credit (overkill for small batch), direct cash via informal channels (no paper trail, no dispute resolution).

**Currency:** Always agree on USD pricing. Workshop converts to VND at their end. Wise and PayPal handle conversion automatically.

**Receipt:** Request a simple invoice (WhatsApp photo of handwritten is fine for samples; typed PDF for batches). Keep all payment confirmations for customs documentation.

## Lead Times

| Order Type | Production Time | Shipping Time (to US) | Total Estimate |
|------------|----------------|----------------------|----------------|
| **1-off custom / sample** | 3-7 days | 7-14 days (DHL/FedEx) | 2-3 weeks |
| **10-unit small batch** | 2-3 weeks | 7-14 days (DHL/FedEx) | 3-5 weeks |
| **25-unit batch** | 3-4 weeks | 10-21 days (sea freight or bulk air) | 5-7 weeks |
| **50-unit batch** | 4-6 weeks | 14-30 days (sea freight recommended) | 6-10 weeks |

**Key factors affecting lead time:**
- Leather availability (if workshop needs to source specific leather, add 3-5 days)
- Vietnamese holidays (Tet/Lunar New Year = 2-3 week shutdown in Jan/Feb)
- Custom hardware (if not standard stock, sourcing adds 1-2 weeks)
- Rainy season (Sep-Dec in central Vietnam) can delay leather drying for some processes

## Shipping Options

| Method | Cost Estimate (10 units) | Transit Time | Best For |
|--------|--------------------------|-------------|----------|
| **DHL Express** | $80-150 | 5-7 days | Samples, urgent small batches |
| **FedEx International** | $70-130 | 5-8 days | Same as DHL, compare rates |
| **EMS (Vietnam Post)** | $30-60 | 10-21 days | Budget-friendly small shipments |
| **Sea Freight (LCL)** | $200-400 (shared container) | 25-35 days | 50+ units, not time-sensitive |

**Shipping notes:**
- Workshop typically packages and drops off at DHL/FedEx in Da Nang (multiple offices available)
- For larger batches, consider a freight forwarder in Da Nang (workshop delivers to forwarder's warehouse)
- Insure all shipments — leather goods are high-value, low-weight
- Request photos of packed shipment before dispatch
- Include commercial invoice + packing list in shipment for US customs

**US Import Duties:**
- Leather handbags/bags: HTS 4202.21 — duty rate ~8-10%
- Merchandise Processing Fee: 0.3464% of declared value (min $33.58, max $651.50)
- No additional tariffs under current US-Vietnam trade status (MFN/NTR)
- Declare accurate values — under-declaration risks customs seizure
