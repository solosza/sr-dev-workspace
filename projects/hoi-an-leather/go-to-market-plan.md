# Go-to-Market Plan: Hoi An Leather Goods

## Executive Summary

We sell handmade, full-grain leather bags crafted by a family workshop in Da Nang/Hoi An — but shipped from US inventory in 3-5 days, eliminating the 2-4 week wait that every competitor forces on American buyers. The product competes on convenience and authenticity, not price: $89-$249 retail at 55%+ gross margins, with break-even at just 14-15 units per 25-unit batch. Three SKUs (tote, crossbody, duffel) form a pricing ladder that acquires customers at $89 and upgrades them to $249.

---

## Phase 1: Test with Existing Inventory

**Goal:** Validate demand with zero production risk. Sell bags already in hand from prior Vietnam trips.

**Timeline:** Weeks 1-6

| Step | Action | Cost | Notes |
|------|--------|------|-------|
| 1 | Photograph each bag (phone camera, natural light) | $0 | Front, back, side, interior, detail shots |
| 2 | Measure and weigh each bag, create spec sheet | $0 | Dimensions, strap length, leather type |
| 3 | Open Etsy shop, add workshop as Production Partner | $0 | Required for compliance — disclose before listing |
| 4 | List each bag with optimized titles and all 13 tags | $0.20/listing | Title formula: Primary keyword + material + style + origin |
| 5 | Ship orders via USPS Priority Mail, branded packaging | $13-21/order | Mailer box + dust bag + leather care card |
| 6 | Order 2-3 samples from workshop via DHL Express | $45-65/bag + $50-80 shipping | Prototypes for next SKUs, validate workshop quality |

**Phase 1 success criteria:**
- 5+ sales in first 6 weeks
- 1+ five-star review
- Workshop sample quality matches or exceeds existing inventory

**Phase 1 investment:** ~$150-300 (samples + shipping + listing fees)

## Phase 2: First Import Batch

**Goal:** Place a 25-unit production order across 3 SKUs. Build real inventory for sustained sales.

**Timeline:** Weeks 7-14 (overlaps with Phase 1 sales)

| Step | Action | Timeline | Cost |
|------|--------|----------|------|
| 1 | Finalize spec sheets for 3 SKUs (crossbody, duffel, tote) | Week 7 | $0 — use spec sheet template from supplier-terms.md |
| 2 | Send specs to workshop via WhatsApp, confirm pricing | Week 7-8 | Production: $40/crossbody, $80/duffel, $25/tote |
| 3 | Pay 50% deposit via Wise | Week 8 | ~$500-525 deposit |
| 4 | Workshop produces 25 units (3-4 week lead time) | Weeks 8-12 | Photo approval at midpoint |
| 5 | Pay remaining 50%, workshop ships via DHL Express | Week 12 | $270-450 shipping, DHL handles customs |
| 6 | Receive, inspect all units, photograph new inventory | Week 13 | Duty: ~8.5% = $60-90 |
| 7 | List on Etsy, begin selling from US stock | Week 14 | $0.20/listing |

**Landed cost per unit (25-unit batch):**

| SKU | Production | Freight | Duty + MPF | Landed COGS | Retail Price | Gross Margin |
|-----|-----------|---------|------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Crossbody | $40.00 | $14.50 | $3.54 | $58.04 | $129 | 55.0% |
| Duffel | $80.00 | $23.00 | $6.68 | $109.68 | $249 | 55.9% |
| Tote | $25.00 | $11.00 | $2.22 | $38.22 | $89 | 57.1% |

**Suggested batch mix (25 units):** 10 crossbody, 5 duffel, 10 tote
- Total batch investment: ~$1,450 (production) + ~$350 (shipping) + ~$125 (duty) = ~$1,925
- Break-even: sell 14-15 of 25 units (56-60%)
- Full-batch profit: ~$1,500-2,000

**Payment method:** Wise (TransferWise) — 1-2% fees, 1-2 business day transfer, workshop receives VND directly.

## Phase 3: Scale

**Triggers — enter Phase 3 when ANY of these are met:**
- 30+ orders/month sustained for 2 consecutive months
- 15+ Etsy sales completed (demand proven)
- $1,500+/month Etsy revenue (justifies Shopify subscription)
- Approaching $10K trailing-year Etsy revenue (offsite ads threshold)

**Timeline:** Month 4-12

### 3.1 Add Shopify (Month 3-4)

| Action | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Launch Shopify Basic ($39/month) | Own your brand, build email list, lower per-sale fees |
| Install Etsy-Shopify inventory sync (LitCommerce or CedCommerce) | Prevent overselling across channels |
| Build brand storytelling pages (artisan story, workshop photos, leather sourcing) | Convert browsers to buyers |
| Start email capture (10% off first order) | Build owned audience for repeat purchases |

### 3.2 Transition to 3PL (Month 4-6)

| Volume | Fulfillment | Monthly Cost |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| <30 orders/month | Self-fulfill from home | $0 fixed + $13-21/order |
| 30-100 orders/month | ShipMonk | $250/month minimum + per-order fees |
| 100+ orders/month | ShipBob | $275/month minimum, multi-warehouse 2-day shipping |

### 3.3 Increase Order Volume (Month 6+)

| Batch Size | Freight Method | Per-Unit Freight | Lead Time |
|------------|---------------|-----------------|-----------|
| 25 units | DHL Express | $11-18/unit | 5-8 days |
| 50 units | Air cargo (freight forwarder) | $5-10/unit | 7-14 days |
| 100+ units | Sea freight (LCL) | $2-4/unit | 35-45 days |

**Sea freight becomes viable at 100+ units** — below that, air freight wins on cost when factoring per-CBM minimums, customs broker fees ($150-250/entry), and cash flow delay.

### 3.4 Add Social Commerce (Month 6-12)

- Instagram Shop + TikTok Shop — storytelling-first content (workshop videos, leather selection process, artisan profiles)
- Paid ads on Shopify traffic (not Etsy — Etsy's mandatory 12% offsite ads fee at $10K+ makes their ads uncontrollable)

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|------------|
| **Quality inconsistency at scale** | Returns, bad reviews, brand damage | Medium | Spec sheets with exact measurements + photo approval at production midpoint + inspect every unit on arrival. First batch is the quality gate — don't reorder until satisfied. |
| **Returns and refunds** | Lost revenue, shipping costs both ways | Medium | Offer exchange before refund. Photograph every bag before shipping (proves condition). Build 5% return rate into margin model. |
| **Supplier communication gaps** | Wrong specs, delays, misunderstandings | Medium | Use WhatsApp with photo/video for all specs. Send reference photos, not just measurements. Confirm every detail in writing before production starts. |
| **Shipping cost increases** | Margin compression | High (DHL raised rates 5.9% in Jan 2026) | If freight rises 20%, margins drop 1-3 points (still >52%). Maximum sustainable price increase: $5-6 per SKU to restore 55% margin. |
| **Etsy algorithm changes** | Traffic drops overnight | Medium | Shopify as backup channel by Month 3. Email list captures customers off-platform. Never depend on a single channel. |
| **Etsy mandatory offsite ads (12%)** | Margin hit on attributed sales above $10K | Certain (once threshold hit) | Shift volume to Shopify before hitting $10K trailing-year Etsy revenue. Keep Etsy as discovery channel, not primary revenue. |
| **Customs/duty increases** | Higher landed COGS | Low (MFN rates stable) | De minimis already suspended — we budget 8.5-9.5% duty on every shipment. No Section 301 tariffs on Vietnam currently. Monitor trade policy. |
| **Vietnamese holiday shutdown (Tet)** | 2-3 week production halt in Jan/Feb | Certain (annual) | Order 6-8 weeks before Tet. Stock up in November/December for Q1 inventory. |
| **Competitor copies US-stock model** | Lose speed advantage | Low (requires capital + logistics) | First-mover advantage in reviews, SEO ranking, and brand trust. Build a brand, not just a logistics arbitrage. |

**Never discount below 45% gross margin.** Maximum sustainable promotional discount: 15% off retail.

## Decision Log

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **Launch platform** | Etsy first, add Shopify at Month 3 | Zero upfront cost, 95M active buyers, fastest time to first sale (1-4 weeks). Shopify adds brand ownership and lower fees once volume justifies $39/month. |
| **Fulfillment path** | Self-fulfill Phase 1-2, 3PL at 30+ orders/month | 3PL minimums ($250-275/month) are a tax on low volume. Self-fulfillment allows full QC, branded packaging, and handwritten notes — brand-building that 3PLs can't replicate. |
| **Freight method** | DHL Express for Phase 1-2 | Door-to-door, customs brokerage included, 5-8 day transit. Air cargo at 50+ units, sea freight at 100+ units. |
| **Payment to workshop** | Wise (TransferWise) | 1-2% fees, mid-market exchange rate, 1-2 day transfer, workshop receives VND directly. PayPal for first sample only (4-5% fees but more familiar). |
| **Pricing strategy** | Premium pricing ($89-$249), compete on convenience + authenticity | We are NOT the cheapest option. Vietnam-shipped competitors are $40-160 but 2-4 weeks slow. We match US domestic brand pricing (Madewell, Cuyana) while offering the artisan origin story. |
| **SKU ladder** | Tote ($89) → Crossbody ($129) → Duffel ($249) | Entry-level tote acquires customers. Crossbody is the volume driver. Duffel anchors perceived value and makes $129 feel accessible. |
| **Production partner** | Da Nang family workshop | Existing relationship, proven quality from prior purchases, family operation with direct communication. Formalize with spec sheets and QC process before scaling. |
| **Country of origin** | "Made in Vietnam" marked on every bag | CBP requires permanent country-of-origin marking on all imports. Non-negotiable compliance requirement. |
| **Etsy Production Partner disclosure** | Disclose workshop before listing | Etsy policy requires disclosure of all production partners. We design, they produce — fully compliant with Etsy's handmade policy. |
