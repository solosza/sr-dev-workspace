# Pricing Strategy: Landed Cost Model for 3 SKUs

## Landed Cost Table

All costs in USD. Based on a 25-unit import batch via DHL Express from Da Nang.

| Cost Component | Crossbody Bag | Duffel Bag | Tote Bag |
|----------------|---------------|------------|----------|
| **Production (workshop)** | $40.00 | $80.00 | $25.00 |
| **Intl. Freight (DHL, per unit)** | $14.50 | $23.00 | $11.00 |
| **Import Duty** | $3.40 (8.5%) | $6.40 (8%) | $2.13 (8.5%) |
| **MPF (0.35%)** | $0.14 | $0.28 | $0.09 |
| **Landed COGS** | **$58.04** | **$109.68** | **$38.22** |

**Notes:**
- Duty rates: Crossbody = HTS 4202.21 (8-9%, use 8.5%); Duffel = HTS 4202.12 (8%); Tote = HTS 4202.21 (8.5%)
- Freight based on 25-unit DHL Express batch midpoints from logistics-fulfillment.md
- Duffel freight higher due to weight (~1 kg/unit vs ~500g for crossbody/tote)
- De minimis exemption suspended — duty applies to ALL shipments regardless of value

## Retail Price Recommendations

Prices set to achieve >55% gross margin (revenue - landed COGS) / revenue, validated against competitor pricing from market-analysis.md.

| SKU | Recommended Retail | Gross Margin | Competitor Range | Justification |
|-----|-------------------|--------------|------------------|---------------|
| **Crossbody Bag** | **$129** | 55.0% | $50-120 (Vietnam-shipped) | Premium justified by 3-5 day US delivery vs. 2-4 week competitor shipping. At top of range but below Atelier Hoi An ($120+ for small bags, bespoke). |
| **Duffel Bag** | **$249** | 55.9% | $120-160 (Vietnam-shipped) | $89-90 premium over competitor ceiling. Defensible: fast US shipping eliminates 3-4 week wait. Full-grain leather weekender at $249 competes with mainstream US brands (Madewell $228, Cuyana $248). |
| **Tote Bag** | **$89** | 57.1% | $40-100 (Vietnam-shipped) | Mid-range pricing. Fast shipping differentiator. Entry-level SKU to acquire customers who upgrade to crossbody/duffel. |

**Pricing philosophy:** We compete on convenience + authenticity, not on price. Vietnam-based sellers are cheaper but 2-4 weeks slower. We match US domestic brand pricing while offering the artisan story.

## Break-Even Analysis: 25-Unit Import Batch

### Crossbody Bag (25 units)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total batch investment | $1,451 (25 x $58.04) |
| Contribution per sale | $101.54 ($129 - $27.46 variable costs) |
| **Break-even point** | **15 units** (14.3 rounded up) |
| Profit after all 25 sold | $1,088 (25 x $43.50) |
| ROI on batch | 75% |

### Duffel Bag (25 units)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total batch investment | $2,742 (25 x $109.68) |
| Contribution per sale | $204.14 ($249 - $44.86 variable costs) |
| **Break-even point** | **14 units** (13.4 rounded up) |
| Profit after all 25 sold | $2,362 (25 x $94.46) |
| ROI on batch | 86% |

### Tote Bag (25 units)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total batch investment | $956 (25 x $38.22) |
| Contribution per sale | $67.84 ($89 - $21.16 variable costs) |
| **Break-even point** | **15 units** (14.1 rounded up) |
| Profit after all 25 sold | $741 (25 x $29.62) |
| ROI on batch | 78% |

### Summary

All SKUs break even at **14-15 units sold** (56-60% of batch). Remaining 10-11 units are pure profit contribution.

**Worst-case scenario:** If only 15 of 25 units sell, you recover your investment. The remaining 10 units are unsold inventory — not a loss, just delayed revenue.

## Pricing Ladder Strategy

| Tier | SKU | Price | Role |
|------|-----|-------|------|
| Entry | Tote Bag | $89 | Customer acquisition — lowest risk purchase, gateway to brand |
| Core | Crossbody Bag | $129 | Bread-and-butter SKU — highest volume potential |
| Premium | Duffel Bag | $249 | Anchor price — makes $129 crossbody feel accessible |

**Psychological anchoring:** List the duffel first in shop display. The $249 price makes the $129 crossbody feel like a deal. The $89 tote catches browsers who aren't ready for $129+.
