# Platform Decision: Etsy vs Shopify

## Fee Comparison

| Fee Category | Etsy | Shopify (Basic Plan) |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly subscription** | $0 (free to list) | $39/mo ($29/mo annual) |
| **Listing fee** | $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months) | $0 |
| **Transaction fee** | 6.5% of total sale (item + shipping + gift wrap) | 0% (with Shopify Payments) |
| **Payment processing** | 3% + $0.25 per transaction (US sellers) | 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (online) |
| **Offsite ads** | 15% on attributed sales (<$10K/yr, opt-out available); 12% on attributed sales (>$10K/yr, mandatory, permanent) | N/A — you control your own ads |
| **Third-party payment fee** | N/A (Etsy Payments required) | 2.0% surcharge if not using Shopify Payments |

### Effective Fee on a $100 Sale (US, no offsite ads)

| Component | Etsy | Shopify Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 | $0 |
| Transaction fee | $6.50 | $0 |
| Payment processing | $3.25 | $3.20 |
| **Total per sale** | **$9.95 (10.0%)** | **$3.20 (3.2%)** |
| Monthly subscription (amortized over 30 sales) | $0 | $1.30 |
| **Effective total per sale** | **$9.95** | **$4.50** |

**Breakeven analysis:** Shopify becomes cheaper per-sale at ~7 sales/month ($39 subscription / ~$6.75 Etsy fee premium per $100 sale). Below 7 sales/month, Etsy's zero-subscription model is cheaper despite higher per-transaction fees.

## Etsy Production Partner Policy

### Can You Commission Goods from an Overseas Workshop?

**Yes — with disclosure.** Etsy's Production Partner policy explicitly allows sellers to work with manufacturers, including overseas workshops, provided:

1. **You are the original designer.** You must make all creative decisions — materials, aesthetics, dimensions, functionality. The workshop executes your design; they do not design for you.
2. **You disclose every production partner.** Each partner must be added in your Etsy shop settings before manufacturing begins. Etsy automatically adds a disclosure badge to affected listings.
3. **Partners comply with applicable laws.** Labor, safety, and employment laws in the partner's jurisdiction must be followed.

### What Qualifies vs. What Doesn't

| Qualifies | Does NOT Qualify |
|---|---|
| You design a bag, a Hoi An workshop produces it to your specs | You buy pre-designed bags from a workshop and resell them |
| Workshop handles one part of production (e.g., stitching, hardware) | Workshop designs and produces the entire product with no creative input from you |
| You specify materials, dimensions, hardware, thread color | You pick from a catalog of existing designs |

**Source:** [Etsy Production Partner Help Center](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336547-Working-with-Production-Partners-on-Etsy) and [Etsy Seller Handbook — Production Partners](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276487437920)

### Implications for Hoi An Leather

This model fits our use case perfectly. We design the products (bag styles, dimensions, leather type, hardware, stitching patterns) and commission a Hoi An workshop to produce them. As long as we:
- Add the workshop as a Production Partner in Etsy shop settings
- Maintain documentation of our design specifications
- Ensure the workshop meets labor/safety standards

...we are fully compliant with Etsy's handmade policy. This is the same model used by Hoi An Soul Leather and other competitors already selling on Etsy.

## Time to First Sale

| Factor | Etsy | Shopify |
|---|---|---|
| **Built-in traffic** | ~95 million active buyers browsing the marketplace | Zero — you bring your own traffic |
| **Shop setup time** | Hours to 2 days | 1-3 days (more customization needed) |
| **Time to first sale** | 1-4 weeks (with optimized listings and Etsy SEO) | Weeks to months (requires content marketing, social, paid ads, or email list) |
| **Discovery mechanism** | Etsy search algorithm, category browsing, recommendations | Google SEO (slow), paid ads (fast but costly), social media |
| **Day-one visitors** | Possible — Etsy surfaces new listings to test engagement | Zero unless you drive traffic yourself |

**Key insight:** Etsy is a marketplace with built-in demand. Shopify is a storefront — beautiful and brandable, but invisible without marketing spend. For a new seller with no existing audience, Etsy provides the fastest path to first revenue.

## Running Both Simultaneously

### Is It Allowed?

**Yes.** Neither platform prohibits selling on the other. Many successful handmade sellers operate both concurrently. Etsy's terms do not require exclusivity.

### How It Works in Practice

| Concern | Solution |
|---|---|
| **Inventory sync** | Use a Shopify-Etsy integration app (e.g., LitCommerce, CedCommerce, ExportYourStore) to sync inventory automatically. Sale on one platform decreases stock on both. |
| **Order management** | Centralize orders through Shopify's admin or a multi-channel tool. Fulfill from the same US inventory regardless of which platform the order came from. |
| **Pricing** | Can differ slightly — Etsy buyers expect artisanal pricing; Shopify allows brand-premium positioning. Most sellers keep prices within 5-10% of each other. |
| **Branding** | Etsy has limited branding options (banner, logo, about page). Shopify allows full brand expression (custom theme, storytelling pages, email capture). |
| **SEO conflict** | No — Etsy listings and Shopify pages have different URLs and serve different search intents. Both can rank for your product keywords. |

### Revenue Impact

Retailers selling on 3+ channels generate 143% more revenue on average than single-channel sellers. Even 2 channels (Etsy + Shopify) significantly diversifies revenue and reduces platform dependency risk.

## Recommendation: Start on Etsy, Add Shopify at Month 3

### Why Etsy First

1. **Zero upfront cost.** No monthly subscription — you only pay when you sell. This aligns with lean validation: prove demand before investing in infrastructure.
2. **Built-in demand.** 95 million active buyers already searching for "handmade leather bag." No ad spend required to get first eyeballs.
3. **Fastest time to first sale.** 1-4 weeks with optimized listings vs. weeks/months on Shopify. Critical for validating product-market fit quickly.
4. **Production Partner policy fits our model.** Commissioning a Hoi An workshop to produce our designs is explicitly compliant — many competitors already operate this way.
5. **Competitor validation.** Hoi An Soul Leather and Lotusvietnam are already successful on Etsy, proving the market exists for Vietnam-sourced handmade leather on this platform.

### Why Add Shopify at Month 3

1. **Brand ownership.** Etsy limits your brand expression. Shopify lets you build a storytelling-first experience (artisan videos, leather sourcing story, workshop photos).
2. **Lower per-sale fees.** Once you hit ~7 sales/month, Shopify's fee structure is significantly cheaper. At 30 sales/month on $100 items, you save ~$165/month vs. Etsy-only.
3. **Offsite ads risk mitigation.** If you cross $10K revenue on Etsy, the 12% mandatory offsite ads fee kicks in permanently. Having Shopify as an alternative channel limits exposure.
4. **Email list ownership.** Etsy doesn't share customer emails for marketing. Shopify lets you build an owned audience for repeat purchases, launches, and loyalty programs.
5. **Multi-channel revenue boost.** Diversifying across platforms protects against Etsy algorithm changes, policy shifts, or account issues.

### Month 3 Trigger Criteria

Add Shopify when ANY of these are met:
- 15+ Etsy sales completed (proven demand)
- 3+ repeat customer inquiries (brand loyalty signal)
- $1,500+ monthly Etsy revenue (justifies $39/mo Shopify subscription)
- Approaching $10K trailing-year Etsy revenue (offsite ads threshold)

### Long-Term Channel Strategy

| Phase | Timeline | Channels | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | Month 1-3 | Etsy only | Validate demand, optimize listings, collect reviews |
| **Expand** | Month 3-6 | Etsy + Shopify | Build brand site, start email list, sync inventory |
| **Scale** | Month 6-12 | Etsy + Shopify + Instagram/TikTok Shop | Content marketing, social commerce, paid ads on Shopify traffic |
| **Mature** | Year 2+ | Full multi-channel | Shopify becomes primary (lower fees, owned brand), Etsy becomes discovery channel |
