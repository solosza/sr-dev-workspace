# Hoi An Leather Goods — US Import & Sales Business

## Business Concept

Import handmade leather goods from a family workshop in Hoi An / Da Nang, Vietnam and sell to US customers. The supplier relationship is already established — $800 spent in-shop during a personal visit, custom orders fulfilled same-day or next-day via Grab delivery, and direct contact (WhatsApp) is in hand.

## Supplier Context

- **Location:** Family workshop, Da Nang / Hoi An area, Vietnam
- **Quality:** Exceptional handmade leather — duffel bags ~$80 USD (2M dong), custom shoulder bag made in ~24 hours for ~$40 USD (1M dong)
- **Relationship:** 10-15 bags purchased in one visit, personal rapport established
- **Fulfillment:** Same-day or next-day production, delivered via Grab

## Research Scope

This project covers market viability research, supplier formalization, platform selection, import logistics, and pricing strategy. The deliverable is an actionable go-to-market plan ready to execute.
