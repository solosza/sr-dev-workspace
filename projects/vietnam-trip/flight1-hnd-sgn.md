## Recommendation: ANA NH891

**Best value pick: ANA NH891 at ~$255.**

Reasons:
1. **Schedule advantage** — Departs 17:05 on May 12, arrives 21:50 same day. You get a full night's sleep at the hotel and a complete day on May 13. The red-eye alternatives (VietJet, JAL) mean arriving exhausted at 5–6 AM.
2. **Full-service airline** — 2 checked bags + meals included. VietJet's $186 becomes ~$220+ after adding a checked bag and meal, narrowing the gap.
3. **787-10 Dreamliner** — Better cabin pressure, humidity, and comfort vs. VietJet's A330.
4. **Price-to-value ratio** — ~$60 more than VietJet buys a dramatically better experience and schedule.

**Budget pick:** VietJet VJ821 at $186–$194 if cost is the top priority and red-eye is acceptable.
