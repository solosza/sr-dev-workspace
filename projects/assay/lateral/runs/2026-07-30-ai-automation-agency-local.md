# Lateral — Governed automation for regulated local businesses

**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Idea:** the governed-niche reframe.

## The default (what everyone does)
Sell automations TO local businesses on a retainer (the AAA model). Cold outreach, upfront setup fee, monthly retainer.

## The flip (invert the assumption)
**Charge nothing upfront — take a cut of the value you recover.** Rev-share / outcome-only: you get paid *only* from the hours saved or revenue recovered. This flips the whole sales problem — a hard-to-reach, skeptical local SMB has **zero risk to say yes**, which cracks the reachability barrier that kills everyone else. Contrarian to the retainer norm.

## The weird recombination
Governed automation + *own the business*: **operator-owned proof** — actually run or co-own ONE regulated business, fully automated, as the ultimate case study + a data moat no agency has. You're not pitching automation; you're *showing your own numbers.*

## The contrarian bet
**Outcome-only pricing + operator-owned proof.** Remove the buying friction entirely (rev-share) and remove the credibility gap entirely (your own automated business as the demo). Both attack the two things that actually kill this — reachability and trust — from the side, not head-on.
