# Assay - Wrap the assay engine in a UI (sell the output, upsell the loops)

Date: 2026-07-31 - Source: user idea. Ranked on merit (fit is a tag). Plain output rendered as a leaderboard.

## Short version
The instinct is right, but flip the emphasis: the under-$10 check is the hook, the paid deep workup (competition + deep-dive + plan) is the business. And the best buyers may not be $10 dreamers.

## Ranked ways to do it
1. Charge for the deep workup, not the quick check - BUILD - the upsell is the business, lead with it.
2. Sell to incubators / accelerators / investors - TEST FIRST - real budgets, they screen hundreds of ideas, not tire-kickers.
3. The under-$10 idea check for founders - TEST FIRST - great top-of-funnel, but crowded (IdeaBrowser et al.) and cheap buyers rarely act; prove pay + upgrade first.
4. A paid vetted-ideas feed / channel - TEST FIRST - engine feeds a media business (pairs with the check tool as marketing).
5. White-label for agencies - TEST FIRST - fewer customers, bigger contracts.
6. General score-any-decision tool - DON'T BUILD - too broad; focus on ideas first.

## The edge and the catch
Edge: the engine KILLS ideas honestly (unlike the optimism tools) and has the full ladder nobody else productized (find, vet, size up rivals, deep dive, grow). Catch: crowded idea-tool space + $10 buyers browse not build.
