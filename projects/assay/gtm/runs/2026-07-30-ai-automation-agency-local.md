# GTM — Governed [niche] automation

**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Business:** governed automation for a regulated local niche
**Verdict:** Win on a **warm/vertical channel** — cold outreach to local SMBs is the reachability killer; a partner/referral motion sidesteps it entirely.

## Channel (pick 1-2, not all)
- **Vertical partners** — the SaaS/tools the niche already pays for (e.g. the dental PMS), as an implementation/referral partner. Warm, credible, reachable.
- **Niche associations / communities** — where the owners already gather. Referral-led.
*(Not: generic cold email/DM to local SMBs — that's the trap.)*

## The hook
"Compliant [niche] automation with a full audit trail — done-for-you, and a human stays in control of every patient/client action."

## Funnel
warm intro (partner/referral) → demo the governed automation on *their* data → 30-day paid pilot → retain + expand.

## Mechanic + assets
Partner outreach + a one-page case study (the receipts: hours saved, zero compliance incidents). Ask each happy client for 1 referral.

## First-customer test
20-30 warm touches in ONE niche via ONE partner/association. **Pass = 1 paid pilot.** Budget cap: time only (no paid ads yet).

**HITL:** commit the channel · adjust · kill (no warm channel = reconsider).
