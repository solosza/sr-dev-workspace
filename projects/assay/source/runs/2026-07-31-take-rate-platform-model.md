# Source run - the take-rate platform model (from the OnlyFans-founder post)

Date: 2026-07-31 - Seed: the BUSINESS MODEL only (not adult content). Take a percentage of every transaction from a large pool of small earners; profitable from day one; no VC; rails they cannot leave. Ranked on merit, fit as a tag.

## The real read (what the seed hand-waves)
Taking 20% as the middleman is the EASY part. The hard, defensible part is two things: (1) cold-starting liquidity in a supply pool that has no better option, and (2) a trust + rails moat so they cannot leave. OnlyFans got both for free from built-in adult demand + creator desperation. Any new take-rate platform has to earn both. The take-rate is the reward; liquidity + lock-in is the whole game. Also note the seed's own unease ("a cut of their skin"): the extractive framing is a real retention risk, which points at the aligned-incentive reframe (#2).

## Hunters
- trends / why-now: new classes of small earners forming NOW with no dedicated rails (people selling AI agents/automations, micro-services). They cobble Stripe + Gumroad + Discord.
- pain: small earners get gouged, chargeback-hit, and deplatformed (exactly what OF creators fought). Trust + payment + dispute infra is the unmet need.
- arbitrage: the opening is a booming earner category with NO dedicated take-rate platform yet. Be the vertical platform before it consolidates.
- assets: our kernel (audit + attestation + HITL) is a TRUST rail. A take-rate platform whose cut is justified by governance/trust is defensible.
- gaps: existing take-rate platforms under-serve trust (chargebacks, non-delivery, disputes). Solve trust -> justify the cut + retain.
- bookmarks / prior-art: recurring "governed rail" theme (ai-automation-agency venture + last run's reliability-layer idea). Note for /sharpen; not a dedup kill.
