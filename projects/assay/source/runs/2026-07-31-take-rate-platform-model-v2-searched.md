## What the research changed
Armchair ideas (construction payments, generic chargeback recovery) turned out to be ALREADY BUILT. Grounding killed the naive picks - which is the point.

Grounded facts used:
- Model works bootstrapped: Ticket Tailor - $6M ARR, 20 staff, ~19% of Eventbrite's global ticket volume, 100% owner-owned, no VC. Auxpanel (dental vertical) $30-50k MRR, 85% margins, no marketing.
- Creator economy $250B in 2026; creators lose 15-30% to fees; 73% juggle multiple payout tools - BUT payments are consolidating fast (Stripe, Lemonsqueezy, Meta USDC, Rumble crypto) -> top is crowding.
- Freelance take-rates are brutal + hated: Fiverr 20% = $12k/yr on $5k/mo.
- Chargebacks: $20-100/dispute, avg $74 all-in; recovery providers already take 20-25% pay-on-win (Chargeblast, Justt) -> model validated, category owned.
- Construction contractor payments = CROWDED: Adaptive (280 customers), Billd ($7.3M), Buildertrend, Briq, Beam, MakersHub.
- Still-open fragmented B2B (per research): agriculture, specialty chemicals, specialty wholesale (jewelry/home goods/apparel), contract management for 30M SMBs.

## Auto-assayed top 2
- #1 Contingency recovery in an un-automated pool -> Test first. Model is validated (20-25% pay-on-win, buyers love no-win-no-fee). Opening = a pool that is large, recurring, automatable, and NOT already audited. Kill-risk: chargebacks/freight are taken; you must find the un-audited pool. Cheapest test: pick one pool, hand-audit 10 accounts, measure recoverable % and willingness to pay on contingency.
- #2 Fragmented B2B vertical marketplace -> Test first, conditional on the specific niche. Kill-risk: verify the chosen niche is not already being consolidated (the construction lesson). Cheapest test: pick one niche, confirm many small vendors + high transaction value + no dominant platform, then get 10 sellers + a buyer.
