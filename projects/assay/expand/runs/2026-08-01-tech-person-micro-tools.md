# Expand — "The tech person for people who don't have one"

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · idea: `projects/tech-person-business/idea.md` · assay: `projects/assay/runs/2026-08-01-tech-person-micro-tools.md`

## Empire version
Not a tool shop. The operating system for the long tail of work software forgot: a proprietary map of what small operators need, an intake-to-hosting pipe that mass-produces tools, distributed by a franchise of locally-trusted people, monetized as recurring hosting + per-niche product lines, with accumulated demand-data as the moat. The nurse sheet is customer #1.

## Angles that hit (all 7)
| Angle | The play | Upside |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Productize | Proven niche tool (care-team scheduling) -> repeatable self-serve product, $/mo | Build once sell 100x; escape hours-for-dollars |
| Data | Every build = logged "busywork + its fix"; accumulate the taxonomy | Benchmark / demand-signal feed / uncopyable catalog |
| Platform | Intake layer ("describe your annoyance") -> auto-built tool + hosting others build on | Own the pipe + hosting margin; AI does the build |
| License | Franchise the playbook to people who own local trust you don't | Their throughput not your bottleneck; royalty on builds/hosting |
| Stack | Land one tool -> "on-call tech person" plan -> whole small-ops spine | Outsourced tech dept; own the operating spine |
| Distribution | "Made by" footer on every tool + niche aggregator partnerships + build-in-public video | The demos are the marketing; each tool seeds the next |
| Adjacent | Niche-tool marketplace; "tools-as-a-perk" sold to banks/insurers for their SMBs; own one niche's system-of-record | Each a venture the map reveals -> feed /assay |

## Highest-leverage next expansion
Productize the ONE proven niche tool. Turns bespoke labor into an asset and seeds the data + platform + license angles at once.
