# Expand — Governed automation for regulated local businesses

**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Idea:** the governed-niche reframe from the pipeline run.

## The empire version
Don't be an automation agency. Become **the compliance-grade automation *platform + standard* for regulated local businesses.** Win one niche as proof (dental) → **own the data** (the compliance benchmark no one else has) → **productize + platform** the governed layer so others build on your rails → **license/certify** operators across niches. The kernel is the moat; the **data + a "Certified Compliant Automation" standard** are the compounding assets.

| Angle | The play | Upside |

| **/platform** | Own the compliance layer (audit + HITL + templates) others build on — the AAA crowd builds on *your* rails instead of rolling their own compliance | **Biggest** — own the infrastructure of the space |

| **/distribution** | Audit-backed case studies AS the content, per vertical; association partnerships | Trust flywheel |
