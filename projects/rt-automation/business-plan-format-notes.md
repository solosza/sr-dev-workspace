# Business Plan Format — Healthcare / Medical Services Standard

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Source:** SBA.gov business plan template + healthcare startup accelerator templates (Y Combinator, TechStars, FastTracks)  
**Domain:** SNF respiratory therapy compliance software

---

## Canonical SBA Business Plan Structure

Standard format for healthcare business plans follows the SBA template:

1. **Executive Summary** — one-page overview summarizing problem, solution, market, financials, and funding ask
2. **Company Description** — mission, legal structure, location, history, and ownership
3. **Market Analysis** — target market size, industry trends, customer demographics, competitive landscape
4. **Organization & Management** — org structure, key roles, team qualifications
5. **Products & Services** — detailed description of what is offered and how it works
6. **Marketing & Sales Strategy** — customer acquisition, pricing model, distribution channels
7. **Financial Projections** — P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow projections (typically 3-5 years)
8. **Funding Request** — (if applicable) capital needs and use of funds
9. **Appendix** — supporting documents, research citations, contracts, regulatory references

---

## Healthcare-Specific Additions & Emphasis Areas

Healthcare and medical-services business plans typically add or expand:

1. **Regulatory & Compliance Posture** — state/federal licensing, HIPAA requirements (for digital health), FDA/regulatory pathway
2. **Reimbursement Model** — payer mix (Medicare, Medicaid, commercial), billing/coding practices, reimbursement risk
3. **Clinical Evidence or Advisory** — clinical credibility, evidence base, advisory board qualifications
4. **Key Partnerships** — EHR vendors, hospital systems, billing services, care networks (healthcare is partnership-heavy)
5. **Operations & Infrastructure** — IT security, data governance, compliance monitoring, audit trails (not just clinical logistics)

## Applicability to This Venture: SNF RT Compliance Gate (Pilot Phase)

This venture is a pre-submission compliance validation gate for respiratory therapy billing at a single SNF pilot site. Sections apply as follows:

### Full-Depth Sections (Core to the Pitch)
- **Executive Summary** — YES: one-page pitch of the problem (unsupported claims, audit risk) and solution (validation gate with audit ledger)
- **Company Description** — MODIFIED: this is an add-on compliance tool for a single RT partner, not a full billing platform or SNF; describe as specialized software/service model for pilot phase
- **Market Analysis** — YES: SNF respiratory therapy market, billing error risk, denial/clawback exposure, competitive landscape (notably: PointClickCare's free Billing Advisor already available to SNFs)
- **Products & Services** — YES: the application described in non-technical, owner-facing language (validation gate, audit ledger, human sign-off guarantees)
- **Regulatory/Compliance** — YES: essential to this product; SNF billing regulations, FCA liability framework, audit-defensibility value
- **Reimbursement Model** — YES: how RT billing works at SNFs, what coding errors the gate prevents, billing submission control (never automated)
- **Operations & Infrastructure** — YES: audit ledger as operational infrastructure, data handling, human-review workflows

### Light-Touch or Deferred Sections (Pilot-Specific)
- **Organization & Management** — LIGHT: pilot phase with cousin as design partner and first customer; full org structure deferred to growth phase
- **Marketing & Sales Strategy** — LIGHT: pilot-first GTM; champion path through RT/billing staff/facility owner; full go-to-market strategy deferred after proof-of-concept
- **Financial Projections** — SIMPLIFIED: pilot pricing model (flat per-facility SaaS recommended, below the $150-300/bed/month full-EHR range); exact dollar figure owner-to-confirm based on facility bed count and deployment timeline
- **Funding Request** — NOT APPLICABLE: this is a pitch to a cousin for a pilot partnership, not a capital raise
- **Appendix** — LIGHT: research citations (domain-and-compliance.md, market-and-switching.md, go-no-go.md), compliance references, sample audit ledger format; full regulatory/legal docs deferred

## Format Note

The business plan should follow the canonical structure above but adapt language and emphasis to the pilot-phase, single-SNF context. Avoid technical jargon (kernel, 3-layer, deterministic gate, LLM rubric, AST, harness). Refer to the application as "the system," "the solution," or "the application." Keep compliance and human-review guarantees in plain owner language ("a person always reviews and approves before any claim is billed").
