# AI QA Engineer Job Market Research — June 2026

## Top Role Titles

- QA Automation Engineer ($118K avg, $200K+ at senior)
- AI/ML Test Engineer
- SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test)
- Test Automation Architect
- Quality Engineering Lead
- AI QA Specialist

## Most-Demanded Skills

1. **Coding** — 77% of QA postings require coding skills; coders earn $20K–$40K more
2. **Playwright** — top framework, pays 5–15% more than equivalent Selenium roles
3. **Multi-framework** — knowing both Selenium + Playwright earns 15–25% premium
4. **AI Integration** — 58% of enterprises upskilling QA teams in AI tools
5. **CI/CD** — Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI push rates into $61–80/hr range
6. **API Testing** — Postman, REST Assured, Karate add premium when bundled
7. **pytest / Python** — dominant test framework for automation
8. **Compliance Testing** — healthcare, fintech, government sectors value highly

## Market Demand

- QA positions grew 17% over past year (vs 9% for traditional dev roles)
- Employment for testing specialists projected to grow 25% by 2032 (BLS)
- AI-powered testing is the fastest-growing QA specialization
- SDET role increasingly expects AI tool fluency alongside traditional automation

## Top Hiring Industries

- Healthcare (HIPAA compliance, claims processing)
- Fintech (regulatory compliance, transaction testing)
- Enterprise SaaS (scale testing, integration)
- Government (security compliance, ERP testing)
- AI-native companies (LLM evaluation, agent testing)

## Differentiators (What Gets Hired)

- Framework-building experience (not just test-writing)
- Automation at scale (team-wide adoption, center of excellence)
- AI-assisted testing workflows
- Compliance and regulatory testing expertise
- Multi-layer testing (UI + API + DB + integration)
- Production evaluation systems (not just test suites)
