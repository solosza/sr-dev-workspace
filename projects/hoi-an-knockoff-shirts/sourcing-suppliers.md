## 1. China Sourcing — Primary Regions & Channels

### Guangzhou (Guangdong Province)

Guangzhou is the epicenter of replica and wholesale streetwear production. Key districts:

| District / Market | Specialty | Notes |
|-------------------|-----------|-------|
| **Shahe Clothing Market** | Budget-to-mid streetwear, fast fashion | Second-largest wholesale clothing district in Guangzhou. Chaotic, cheap, high-volume. Best stock appears in morning hours. Deals in low-to-medium-end men's and women's apparel. |
| **Baiyun Leather/Apparel District** | Replica streetwear, branded goods | Known for higher-tier replicas. Factories in surrounding Baiyun district produce for DHgate/AliExpress sellers. |
| **Shisanhang (Thirteen Factories)** | Mid-range wholesale | Traditional wholesale hub. More structured than Shahe, slightly higher price points. Good for consistent quality sourcing. |
| **Zhanxi Clothing Market** | Streetwear, sneakers, accessories | Hot spot for streetwear specifically. Vendors carry replica hoodies, tees, tracksuits from trending brands. |

### Online Platforms (China-Based)

| Platform | Type | How to Use | MOQ | Payment |
|----------|------|-----------|-----|---------|
| **1688.com** | B2B wholesale (Chinese Alibaba) | Requires Chinese reading or agent. Factory-direct prices, 30-70% cheaper than DHgate. Search by product photo or keyword. | 50-100 pcs typical | Alipay |
| **Weidian** | Micro-merchant marketplace | Popular in r/fashionreps community. Boutique sellers with curated rep selections. Use agent (Sugargoo, Superbuy, Pandabuy) to purchase. | 1 pc (retail) | WeChat Pay / Agent |
| **Taobao** | Consumer marketplace | Largest selection. Coded keywords to find reps. Use agent for international shipping. | 1 pc (retail) | Alipay / Agent |
| **DHgate** | International B2C marketplace | English interface, buyer protection, direct shipping to US. Higher prices than 1688 but lower friction. | 1 pc | Credit card, PayPal |
| **AliExpress** | International B2C marketplace | Similar to DHgate but more coded listings. "Shark Mouth Brand" = Bape, etc. | 1 pc | Credit card, PayPal |
| **Alibaba** | B2B international | For bulk orders. Negotiate MOQ, request samples. Trade Assurance for payment protection. | 100-500 pcs | Wire transfer, Trade Assurance |

### Shopping Agents (for 1688/Weidian/Taobao)

International buyers can't pay directly on Chinese platforms. Agents bridge the gap:

| Agent | Platforms Supported | How It Works |
|-------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Sugargoo** | Taobao, 1688, Weidian, JD, Tmall | Paste product link → agent buys → QC photos → ship to you. Warehouse consolidation. |
| **Superbuy** | Same | Established agent, slightly higher fees. Good customer service. |
| **Pandabuy** | Same | Popular in rep community. Unified cart across platforms. |
| **CSSBuy** | Same | Budget option, lower service fees. |

**Agent workflow:** Find item → paste link into agent → pay agent (PayPal/card) → agent buys from seller → QC photos sent to you → approve or return → agent ships internationally.

## 2. Vietnam Sourcing — Hoi An & HCMC

### Vietnam Apparel Industry Overview

Vietnam is the world's third-largest garment exporter (~$46B in exports, 2025). Nearly 6,000 factories employ ~2.7 million workers, producing for Nike, Adidas, Uniqlo, H&M, Lululemon, Gap, and Zara. The infrastructure is mature and export-oriented.

### Hoi An — Artisan & Custom Tailoring Hub

Hoi An is famous for its tailor shops — hundreds of them lining the old town streets. The specialization is **custom-made clothing** with fast turnaround (24-48 hours for a suit). Key characteristics:

| Factor | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Specialty** | Custom tailoring, linen clothing, sustainable/eco garments. Artisan-scale production. |
| **MOQ** | Very low — 1 piece minimum. Ideal for samples and prototyping. |
| **Turnaround** | 24-48 hours for simple garments, 3-5 days for complex pieces. |
| **Quality** | High craftsmanship for custom work. Hand-finishing, attention to detail. |
| **Limitation** | Not set up for replica/branded production. Hoi An tailors make custom originals — they're not in the replica supply chain. |
| **Best for** | "Inspired-by" original designs, custom streetwear with artisan positioning, sample development. |

### HCMC (Ho Chi Minh City) — Factory Scale

| Factor | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Specialty** | Cut-and-sew manufacturing, screen printing, embroidery. Factory-scale production. |
| **MOQ** | 200-500 pcs for most factories. Some flexible manufacturers go as low as 50 pcs. |
| **Quality** | Export-grade. Many factories already produce for Western streetwear brands. |
| **Key players** | Thygesen Textile, Thai Son S.P., Dugarco, Project SGN (low MOQ specialist). |
| **Best for** | Bulk production of original designs once samples are validated in Hoi An. |

### Vietnam vs China — Direct Comparison

| Factor | China (Guangzhou) | Vietnam (Hoi An/HCMC) |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Replica availability** | Extensive — entire supply chains built for replicas | Minimal — not a replica hub |
| **Original production** | Yes, but replica market dominates | Yes — strong custom/original capability |
| **MOQ** | 1 pc (retail) to 100+ (wholesale) | 1 pc (Hoi An) to 200+ (HCMC factories) |
| **Cost per unit** | $3-15 (factory), $10-40 (DHgate retail) | $5-20 (factory), $15-50 (custom Hoi An) |
| **Quality consistency** | Variable — must QC heavily | More consistent — export-standard factories |
| **Lead time** | 7-21 days production, 15-30 days shipping | 3-14 days production, 10-25 days shipping |
| **Communication** | Chinese (Mandarin), some English on Alibaba/DHgate | Vietnamese, decent English in Hoi An (tourism) |
| **Payment** | Alipay, WeChat Pay, wire transfer, PayPal (DHgate) | Wire transfer, PayPal, cash (Hoi An) |
| **Brand story potential** | None — "made in China" has no premium positioning | High — "handcrafted in Vietnam" is a selling point |
| **IP risk** | High for replicas | Low if doing original designs |

**Bottom line:** China for replicas, Vietnam for original "inspired-by" designs with artisan brand positioning. The hybrid play: source replica knowledge/trends from China's market, produce original versions in Vietnam.

## 3. Quality Tier Breakdown

The replica market uses a grading system to indicate closeness to the authentic product:

| Grade | Quality Level | Materials | Details Accuracy | Packaging | Price Range (Streetwear) |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Grade A** | Entry-level | Cheap fabrics, thin cotton | Resembles original from a distance only. Wrong fonts, loose stitching, off-color prints. | None or generic bag | $3-8 |
| **Grade AA** | Low-mid | Better cotton weight, basic screen print | Closer branding but visible flaws on inspection. Tags may be wrong. | Basic packaging | $8-15 |
| **Grade AAA** | Mid-tier | Decent materials, proper weight | Good resemblance. Correct logos and colorways but minor details off (tag font, stitch count, label placement). | May include basic tags | $15-30 |
| **1:1** | High-end replica | Materials sourced from same or similar suppliers as originals | Near-identical. Correct stitching, tags, wash labels, hardware. Passes casual inspection. | Full packaging (tags, dust bag, cards) | $30-60 |
| **OG / Mirror** | Top-tier | Highest quality materials available | Virtually indistinguishable from authentic. Same factory or same-spec materials. Only detectable by professional authentication (Entrupy scan, microscope stitching). | Complete authentic-style packaging | $50-100+ |

### Quality Terms Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **QC** | Quality Check — photos of your specific item before shipping. Standard practice on DHgate and through agents. |
| **GL** | Green Light — community term meaning "this looks good, ship it." |
| **RL** | Red Light — "this has flaws, return/exchange it." |
| **W2C** | Where to Cop — "where can I buy this?" |
| **B&S** | Bait and Switch — seller shows one quality, ships another. Major red flag. |
| **Budget batch** | Cheapest version of a rep, usually Grade A-AA. Obvious flaws but wearable. |
| **Top batch** | Best available version, usually 1:1 or OG grade. |
| **Retail pair/piece** | The authentic item, used as reference for QC comparison. |

## 5. Sample Order Checklist

### Before Ordering

- [ ] **Define what you're sampling** — specific brand/style reference, size range, colorway
- [ ] **Choose sourcing channel** — DHgate (easy, protected) vs agent (cheaper, more options) vs direct factory (bulk only)
- [ ] **Set budget** — sample costs typically $15-60/piece + $15-30 shipping
- [ ] **Identify 2-3 sellers** — never single-source your first sample

### Placing the Order

- [ ] **Order 1 piece per seller** — compare quality across sources
- [ ] **Request QC photos** — mandatory before shipping (standard on DHgate and through agents)
- [ ] **Specify size carefully** — Chinese sizing runs 1-2 sizes smaller than US. Always check size charts. Order one size up if unsure.
- [ ] **Note the batch/version** — ask seller which batch (budget, mid, top) you're getting

### Payment Methods by Channel

| Channel | Accepted Payment | Protection Level |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| DHgate | Credit card, PayPal, bank transfer | High — buyer protection, dispute resolution |
| AliExpress | Credit card, PayPal | High — similar to DHgate |
| Alibaba | Wire transfer, Trade Assurance | Medium-High (use Trade Assurance, never wire without it) |
| Agent (Sugargoo, etc.) | PayPal, credit card | Medium — agent acts as intermediary |
| Direct WeChat/WhatsApp | WeChat Pay, Alipay, USDT, Western Union | Low — no buyer protection. Only with established trust. |

### After Receiving

- [ ] **Compare to retail photos** — check stitching, tags, print quality, fabric weight
- [ ] **Document flaws** — photograph everything for future reference
- [ ] **Test wash** — wash once to check for shrinkage, color bleeding, print durability
- [ ] **Rate the seller** — keep a personal scorecard (quality, communication, speed, accuracy)

## 6. Red Flags & Scam Patterns

### Payment Scams

| Red Flag | What It Means |
|----------|--------------|
| **Full upfront payment demanded** | Legitimate suppliers do 30% deposit / 70% on delivery. Full prepay = high scam risk. |
| **Wire transfer only** | No recourse if scammed. Always use platform payment or Trade Assurance. |
| **"Special discount" for off-platform payment** | Seller wants to bypass buyer protection. Never pay outside DHgate/AliExpress/Alibaba. |
| **Pressure for quick decision** | "Price only valid today" or "last stock" — classic high-pressure scam tactic. |

### Quality Scams

| Red Flag | What It Means |
|----------|--------------|
| **Bait and switch (B&S)** | Great sample, garbage bulk order. Always QC every order, not just the first. |
| **Prices way below market** | If AAA rep hoodies are $25 everywhere but one seller offers $8, it's Grade A at best. |
| **No QC photos provided** | Legitimate sellers provide QC photos before shipping. Refusal = red flag. |
| **Stock photos only** | No real product photos in reviews = untested seller. |
| **Factory refuses video call** | Legitimate factories will do a video walkthrough. Refusal suggests they don't have a factory. |

### Shipping & Customs Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Customs seizure** | Branded replicas can be seized. Sellers use "stealth shipping" (remove tags/logos for transit). |
| **Wrong items shipped** | Always QC before shipping. Document with photos. File dispute immediately. |
| **Lost packages** | Use tracked shipping. DHgate/AliExpress cover lost packages under buyer protection. |
| **Declared value too high** | Seller declares retail value → you pay import duty. Ask seller to declare low ($10-15). |

## 7. Supplier Outreach Template

### For WeChat / WhatsApp (China Suppliers)

```
Hi [Seller Name],

I found your store on [DHgate/1688/Weidian]. I'm interested in ordering
samples of streetwear items — specifically [hoodies/tees/tracksuits] in
[brand reference or style].

Questions:
1. What quality grade do you offer? (AAA / 1:1 / OG?)
2. Can you send QC photos before shipping?
3. What's the sample price for 1-3 pieces?
4. What's your MOQ for bulk orders?
5. Shipping time to US?
6. Payment methods accepted?

I'm a new buyer looking to test quality before placing larger orders.
Looking forward to working together.

[Your Name]
```

```
Subject: Sample Inquiry — Streetwear [Hoodies/Tees] — US Buyer

Hi,

I'm sourcing [product type] for the US market. Looking for:
- Style: [oversized hoodies / graphic tees / tracksuits]
- Material: [380gsm cotton / French terry / etc.]
- Quantity: Starting with 3-5 samples, potential bulk order of [X] pcs
- Customization: [custom prints / original designs / specific colorways]

Please provide:
1. Sample pricing and lead time
2. Bulk MOQ and per-unit pricing
3. Available materials and GSM options
4. Factory photos or video tour
5. Payment terms (Trade Assurance preferred)

Thank you,
[Your Name]
```
