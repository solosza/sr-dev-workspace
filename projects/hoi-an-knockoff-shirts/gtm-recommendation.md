# Go-to-Market Recommendation: Hoi An Streetwear

**Pipeline:** 114 — Hoi An Knockoff Shirts
**Date:** 2026-06-01

## Recommended Path: Private Label Original Streetwear

**Risk Rating: LOW**

Build an original streetwear brand that captures the aesthetic demand driving the replica market — oversized fits, bold graphics, earth tones, vintage washes — without copying any trademarked marks, logos, or proprietary designs. Source production from Vietnam (Hoi An for prototyping, HCMC factories for scale). Sell on Depop + TikTok Shop + Shopify.

This is Tier 4 from the legal-compliance analysis. Every other tier carries legal exposure that exceeds the margin opportunity.

### Why This Path Wins

| Factor | Private Label | Replica Import | Inspired-By Dupe |
|--------|--------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Legal risk | None | Criminal + civil | Trade dress lawsuits |
| Platform access | All platforms | None (all ban replicas) | Self-hosted only |
| CBP seizure risk | Zero | Very high (90%+ seizure rate in mail) | Low but non-zero |
| Margin (tee at $27) | 32-47% net | 47% net (if not seized) | 40-47% net |
| Brand equity | Compounding asset | Zero (disposable) | Limited |
| Scalability | Unlimited | Capped by enforcement | Capped by legal vigilance |
| Startup cost | $600-1,850 | $500-1,000 | $2,000-5,000 (needs IP attorney) |

The replica import path has a 40-75% cost advantage per unit, but a single CBP seizure ($300+ in lost inventory) or a single platform ban (lost storefront + listing history) wipes out months of margin. The math only works if nothing goes wrong — and the enforcement data says things go wrong constantly (32M+ counterfeit goods seized in FY2024 alone).

## Rejected Paths

### Tier 1: Branded Replicas — REJECTED (Risk: CRITICAL)

Importing and reselling items with fake brand logos (Nike, Supreme, Bape, etc.).

**Why rejected:**
- Criminal penalties: up to $2M fine + 10 years prison (first offense)
- Civil statutory damages: $200K-$2M per mark per product type
- CBP seizes 90%+ of counterfeit goods in international mail — the exact channel small e-commerce uses
- Every major marketplace (Depop, eBay, TikTok Shop, Etsy, Poshmark) actively bans replicas with automated detection
- No payment processor will knowingly service counterfeit sales long-term
- Zero brand equity — you're building on someone else's trademark, which can be enforced at any time

**Bottom line:** The risk/reward is catastrophically bad. Even if margins are high per unit, one enforcement action (legal, customs, or platform) destroys the entire operation.

### Tier 2: Unbranded Knockoffs — REJECTED (Risk: HIGH)

Copying exact designs/silhouettes but removing brand logos.

**Why rejected:**
- Trade dress infringement is still actionable — Lululemon sued Costco in 2025 for design copying alone (no logo)
- CBP still flags recognizable designs even without logos
- Marketplace policies flag items that are "too similar" to branded goods
- Margins (50-70%) don't justify the legal defense costs ($10K+ per trade dress claim)
- No brand story — you're still selling a commodity with no defensible positioning

### Tier 3: "Inspired-By" Dupes — REJECTED AS PRIMARY PATH (Risk: MODERATE)

Creating products with similar aesthetic but distinct enough design to avoid trademark claims.

**Why rejected as primary path:**
- The "likelihood of confusion" test is subjective — what you think is "sufficiently differentiated," a brand's legal team may not
- Requires $2-5K IP attorney review before every product launch
- Self-hosted platforms only (Shopify, personal site) — marketplaces will flag items that look too close
- Constant legal vigilance required; one design that crosses the line exposes the entire brand
- Better as a design philosophy within the private label path than as a standalone strategy

## First 5 Launch Actions

### Action 1: Define Brand Identity (Week 1)

Pick a brand name, design a logo, and define the visual language (color palette, typography, graphic style). The brand should evoke the Hoi An/Vietnam artisan story — "designed in [your city], made in Vietnam." Budget: $100-500 (Fiverr logo + self-directed brand deck).

**Deliverable:** Brand name, logo file, 3-5 graphic concepts for first drop.

### Action 2: Order Samples from 3 Sources (Week 1-2)

Order test pieces from three different channels to compare quality and cost:
1. **Vietnam factory** (via Cosmo Sourcing or direct Hoi An tailor contact) — 2-3 custom pieces with your original design
2. **US blank + domestic print** (Bella+Canvas 3001 + DTG via Printful or local print shop) — 2-3 pieces
3. **China factory** (via DHgate or 1688 agent) — 2-3 unbranded blanks to compare fabric quality

Budget: $150-250 total. Purpose: compare fabric, stitching, print quality, and lead times across sources.

**Deliverable:** 6-9 sample pieces in hand, quality comparison scorecard.

### Action 3: Set Up Depop + TikTok Shop Storefronts (Week 2-3)

Register seller accounts on both platforms. Depop has 0% commission (US sellers) and a streetwear-native audience. TikTok Shop has 6% referral fee but algorithmic content discovery.

- Depop: list 3-5 items with styled product photos
- TikTok Shop: create seller account, prepare 2-3 product videos (styling, making-of, fabric close-ups)
- Shopify: set up basic store ($39/mo) as the owned-channel backup

**Deliverable:** Live storefronts on Depop + TikTok Shop, Shopify store ready.

### Action 4: Place First Production Order — 36 Units (Week 3-4)

Based on sample quality results, place a 36-unit batch with the best source. Recommended: 24 T-shirts (3 designs x 8 units in S/M/L/XL) + 12 hoodies (2 designs x 6 units in S/M/L).

- If Vietnam quality won: order from HCMC factory (MOQ 50, negotiate 36 for first run) or Hoi An tailor for premium positioning
- If US blank + print won: order Bella+Canvas blanks + screen print locally (MOQ 24-36 for screen print)

Budget: $300-500 (tees) + $200-350 (hoodies) = $500-850.

**Deliverable:** 36 units in hand, photographed, and listed on all platforms.

### Action 5: Launch Content Engine (Week 4-5)

Create 10-15 pieces of content for TikTok and cross-post to Instagram Reels:
- "How we make our streetwear in Vietnam" (sourcing story)
- Styling videos (outfit builds with the pieces)
- "Streetwear under $30" positioning videos
- Behind-the-scenes of the Hoi An/Vietnam production
- Customer/friend try-on reactions

Post 1-2x daily for 2 weeks. TikTok Shop rewards consistent posting with algorithmic distribution.

**Deliverable:** 10-15 published videos, initial sales data, engagement metrics.

## Decisions Required (Only You Can Decide)

These are questions the research cannot answer — they depend on your personal situation, risk tolerance, and goals.

### 1. Risk Tolerance

**Question:** Are you willing to accept ANY legal risk, or do you want zero IP exposure?

- If **zero risk:** Go pure private label (Tier 4). Original designs only. Higher per-unit cost but no legal overhead.
- If **some risk tolerance:** The "inspired-by" approach (Tier 3) within a private label brand offers higher margins but requires IP attorney review ($2-5K) and constant design vigilance.
- **Not recommended at any risk tolerance:** Tiers 1-2 (branded replicas, unbranded knockoffs).

### 2. Starting Capital

**Question:** How much can you invest upfront?

| Budget | Recommended Approach |
|--------|---------------------|
| $500 or less | Print-on-demand only (Printful/Gooten). Zero inventory. Lower margins (22-35% net) but zero risk. |
| $500-1,000 | 36-unit batch from Vietnam or domestic blanks. Self-fulfill. Break-even in 4-6 weeks. |
| $1,000-2,000 | 72-unit batch + professional brand design + initial ad spend. Faster growth. |
| $2,000+ | Multiple SKUs, professional photography, paid TikTok ads, IP attorney consult. Full launch. |

### 3. Time Commitment

**Question:** Is this a side project (5-10 hrs/week) or a primary focus (20+ hrs/week)?

- **Side project:** Self-fulfill, focus on Depop (lowest time investment), post TikTok content 3x/week
- **Primary focus:** Multi-platform, aggressive content calendar (daily posting), direct supplier relationships, faster iteration on designs

### 4. Target Volume (First 6 Months)

**Question:** What's your sales velocity goal?

| Goal | Implications |
|------|-------------|
| 5-10 units/week | Comfortable side income ($200-400/week profit). Self-fulfill easily. One batch per month. |
| 20-50 units/week | Needs consistent content, multiple platforms, ad spend. Reorder every 2-3 weeks. Consider 3PL at 200+/month. |
| 100+ units/week | Full business operations. HCMC factory relationship required. 3PL mandatory. $5K+ monthly COGS. |

### 5. Vietnam Connection

**Question:** Do you have or can you build a direct relationship with Vietnam-based producers?

- **Yes (personal trip or contact):** Huge advantage. Direct factory relationships cut out agents/middlemen. Hoi An tailors can prototype custom designs in 24-48 hours. The "made in Vietnam" brand story becomes authentic.
- **No:** Use Cosmo Sourcing (Vietnam freight/sourcing specialist) or Alibaba factories with Trade Assurance. Still viable but less differentiated.

### 6. Brand Positioning

**Question:** Which positioning resonates with you?

| Position | Description | Price Point | Audience |
|----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Budget streetwear** | Cheapest option, volume play | $15-22 | Price-sensitive teens, rep buyers switching to legal |
| **Mid-range original** | Quality blanks, original graphics, artisan story | $25-35 tee, $45-55 hoodie | Streetwear enthusiasts who want the look, not the logo |
| **Premium artisan** | Hoi An-made, limited runs, craft narrative | $40-60 tee, $70-100 hoodie | Design-conscious buyers, ethical fashion crowd |

Research supports **mid-range original** as the sweet spot — it fills the gap between $10-15 DHgate reps and $100-500 authentic brands while maintaining 47-57% net margins.

## Final Recommendation

**Go with private label.** The replica underground is a race to the bottom with escalating legal risk. The "inspired-by" path is viable but requires constant legal overhead. Private label captures the same customer (they want the aesthetic, not the logo) with zero legal risk, full platform access, and a brand that compounds in value over time.

The Hoi An connection is the differentiator. "Handcrafted streetwear from Vietnam" is a brand story that no DHgate seller can replicate. Lean into it.

Start small (36 units, $750), validate fast (Depop + TikTok Shop), and scale what works.
