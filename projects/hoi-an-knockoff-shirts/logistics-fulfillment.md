# Logistics & Fulfillment: Import Duties and Freight

## Overview

This document provides the **logistics cost model** for importing small-batch streetwear apparel from Vietnam and China to the US market. It covers HTS classification and duty rates, freight options for small batches, the de minimis threshold (now suspended), Customs seizure risk, and fulfillment strategy. These numbers feed directly into the pricing strategy task.

## 1. US HTS Codes and Duty Rates

### Chapter 61 — Knitted or Crocheted Apparel

| Product | HTS Code | Description | MFN Duty Rate | Vietnam Effective | China Effective |
|---------|----------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Cotton T-shirt | 6109.10.00.27 | T-shirts, singlets, tank tops, knitted, cotton, men's | 16.5% | 16.5% | 16.5% + 7.5% Section 301 = **24.0%** |
| Synthetic T-shirt | 6109.90.10.45 | T-shirts, knitted, man-made fibers, men's | 32.0% | 32.0% | 32.0% + 7.5% Section 301 = **39.5%** |
| Cotton hoodie | 6110.20.20.25 | Pullovers, sweatshirts, knitted, cotton, men's | 16.5% | 16.5% | 16.5% + 7.5% Section 301 = **24.0%** |
| Synthetic hoodie | 6110.30.30.41 | Pullovers, sweatshirts, knitted, man-made fibers, men's | 32.0% | 32.0% | 32.0% + 7.5% Section 301 = **39.5%** |

### Chapter 62 — Woven (Not Knitted) Apparel

| Product | HTS Code | Description | MFN Duty Rate | Vietnam Effective | China Effective |
|---------|----------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Cotton woven shirt | 6205.20.20.10 | Men's shirts, woven, cotton | 19.7% | 19.7% | 19.7% + 7.5% Section 301 = **27.2%** |
| Synthetic woven shirt | 6205.30.20.10 | Men's shirts, woven, man-made fibers | 28.6% | 28.6% | 28.6% + 7.5% Section 301 = **36.1%** |

### Key Classification Notes

- **Knit vs. woven matters.** Chapter 61 (knit) covers T-shirts, hoodies, and most streetwear. Chapter 62 (woven) covers button-up shirts and structured garments. Misclassification triggers penalties.
- **Fiber content matters.** Cotton items carry 16.5-19.7% duty; synthetic items carry 28.6-32.0%. Blended fabrics classify by the fiber with the greatest weight percentage.
- **Section 301 tariffs apply only to China-origin goods.** Vietnam-origin goods avoid this 7.5% surcharge entirely, making Vietnam the clear sourcing advantage for duty optimization.
- **Country of origin is determined by "substantial transformation."** Cutting and sewing in Vietnam from Chinese fabric = Vietnam origin. Printing in Vietnam on a Chinese-made blank = still China origin (printing alone is not substantial transformation).

## 2. De Minimis Threshold — Section 321

### Status as of 2026: SUSPENDED

As of **August 29, 2025**, the US suspended the $800 de minimis duty-free threshold for all countries. A February 2026 executive order continued this suspension indefinitely.

### What This Means for Small-Batch Apparel

| Before (Pre-Aug 2025) | After (Current) |
|----------------------|-----------------|
| Shipments under $800 entered duty-free | ALL shipments subject to full duty + formal entry |
| No formal Customs entry required | Formal entry required regardless of value |
| Minimal documentation | Full HTS classification, commercial invoice, packing list required |
| Self-clearance possible | Customs broker recommended for all entries |

### Impact on This Project

- **The de minimis exemption is no longer viable** for commercial apparel imports at any quantity.
- A 12-unit T-shirt shipment ($48 FOB) previously entered duty-free. Now it faces ~$8 in duties + $20-40 in Customs broker/entry fees — adding $2-4 per unit in landed cost.
- **Formal entry fees** ($20-40 per entry via broker) are now the dominant cost for micro-batches, exceeding the duty itself.
- Strategy: **consolidate shipments** to amortize entry fees. Ship 36+ units per entry minimum.

## 3. Freight Costs: Air and Sea

|------------|-------|-------------|-----------------|----------|--------------|
| Small | 12 T-shirts (~3 kg) | 4 kg (volumetric) | $36-57 | $3.00-4.75 | 3-5 days |
| Medium | 36 T-shirts (~9 kg) | 12 kg (volumetric) | $72-114 | $2.00-3.17 | 3-5 days |

**Rate basis:** $6.00-9.50/kg for small parcels from Asia to US (2026 rates). Volumetric weight often exceeds actual weight for apparel due to bulky packaging.

| Batch Size | Est. Volume | Sea Freight Cost | Per Unit (T-shirt) | Transit Time |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|--------------|
| 36 T-shirts | 0.1 CBM | $80-120 (LCL minimum) | $2.22-3.33 | 18-25 days |

**Note:** LCL shipments have minimum charges ($80-120) regardless of actual volume. At 36 units, air and sea costs per unit are comparable — sea only becomes cost-effective at 100+ units.

Express courier is the most expensive option but handles customs clearance automatically (DDP — Delivered Duty Paid). Best for initial samples, not production runs.

## 4. Customs Seizure Risk

### Risk by Product Type

| Product Type | Seizure Risk | Reason |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Branded replica (visible logos) | **VERY HIGH** | CBP actively targets counterfeit apparel; IP holders file recordations |
| Unbranded knockoff (similar design, no logos) | **LOW** | No trademark violation; design alone is not protectable for most apparel |
| Inspired-by / private label | **NEGLIGIBLE** | Original branding; no IP conflict |

### CBP Enforcement Context

- In FY2023, CBP seized $2.7 billion in counterfeit goods. Apparel and accessories were the #2 category (after consumer electronics).
- **Small quantities do NOT reduce risk.** CBP's e-commerce enforcement unit processes parcels regardless of size. A single parcel with a Nike swoosh on a non-Nike shirt can trigger seizure + penalty.
- Seized goods are destroyed. The importer receives a seizure notice and has 30 days to petition (rarely successful for counterfeits).
- **Vietnam-origin goods face less scrutiny** than China-origin for counterfeiting specifically, but CBP enforcement is origin-agnostic for trademark violations.

### Recommendation

**Ship only unbranded or private-label goods.** The duty savings from small-batch imports are wiped out entirely by a single seizure. This aligns with the legal-compliance.md recommendation (Tier 4: private label).

## 5. Freight Forwarders for Small-Batch Apparel

### Recommended Options

| Forwarder | Best For | Minimum | Key Feature |
|-----------|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Freightos** | Price comparison | No minimum | Marketplace model — compare dozens of forwarder rates; instant quotes; book online |
| **Flexport** | Full-service | ~20 containers/month for dedicated service | Platform-based; Vietnam warehouse hub (Thu Dau Mot); good for scaling up |
| **Cosmo Sourcing** | Vietnam-specific | No minimum | Vietnam freight specialist; handles small batches; sourcing + shipping combined |
| **Alibaba Logistics / Cainiao** | China small-batch | No minimum | Integrated with Alibaba orders; DDP option; automated customs |

### Small-Batch Reality

For 12-36 unit batches, traditional freight forwarders (Flexport, Shapiro) are **overkill** — their value emerges at 100+ units or regular monthly shipments. At small scale:

1. **Use Freightos** to compare LCL/air rates from multiple forwarders
2. **Use supplier's shipping** if buying from Alibaba (Cainiao logistics handles door-to-door)
3. **Use express courier** (DHL/FedEx) for the first 2-3 test orders to validate product quality before committing to freight

## 6. Fulfillment Options

### Option A: Self-Fulfill (Home/Garage)

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|-----------|
| **Startup cost** | $0-200 (shipping supplies, scale, label printer) |
| **Per-order cost** | USPS flat rate: $8-15 per domestic order |
| **Minimum volume** | None |
| **Pros** | Zero fixed cost; full control over packaging/branding; instant start |
| **Cons** | Time-intensive; doesn't scale past ~50 orders/week; no professional unboxing experience |
| **Best for** | First 0-100 orders; validating product-market fit |

### Option B: 3PL (Third-Party Logistics)

| Provider | Monthly Minimum | Setup Fee | Pick-Pack Fee | Storage | Best For |
|----------|----------------|-----------|--------------|---------|----------|
| **ShipMonk** | ~250 orders/month | $0 | $3.00-4.50/order | $25/pallet/month | Apparel-focused; dedicated apparel facility; photo proof |
| **ShipBob** | No hard minimum (Growth Plan targets 400+/month) | $975 implementation | $3.50-5.00/order | $40/pallet/month | DTC brands; fast domestic shipping; easy onboarding |
| **Fulfyld** | No minimum | Varies | $2.50-4.00/order | $20/pallet/month | Small brands; lower minimums; flexible |

**3PL Reality Check:** At 36-unit batches selling 5-10 units/week, monthly order volume is ~20-40 orders — far below ShipMonk's 250 or ShipBob's target of 400. **3PL is not viable until consistent 200+ orders/month.**

### Option C: Supplier Direct Ship (Dropship from Vietnam/China)

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|-----------|
| **Startup cost** | $0 |
| **Per-order cost** | $8-15 shipping (supplier ships direct to customer) |
| **Minimum volume** | Depends on supplier willingness |
| **Pros** | Zero inventory risk; no upfront batch purchase; no warehousing |
| **Cons** | 10-20 day delivery to US customer; no quality control after production; customs on every order (post-de minimis suspension); $20-40 entry fee per shipment destroys margins |
| **Best for** | NOT recommended post-de minimis suspension — per-order customs costs make this unviable |

### Recommended Fulfillment Path

| Phase | Volume | Strategy |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Phase 1: Validation** (0-100 orders) | 1-3 batches of 36 units | Self-fulfill from home; ship via USPS/UPS |
| **Phase 2: Growth** (100-500 orders/month) | Monthly imports of 100-200 units | Self-fulfill or transition to Fulfyld (low minimum 3PL) |
| **Phase 3: Scale** (500+ orders/month) | Bi-weekly imports of 200+ units | ShipMonk (apparel-optimized) or ShipBob |

## 8. Key Decisions for Pricing Strategy

1. **Source from Vietnam, not China** — saves 7.5% Section 301 tariff on every unit; lower seizure scrutiny
2. **Stick to cotton** — 16.5% duty vs. 32% for synthetics; cotton is preferred for streetwear anyway
3. **Ship 36+ units per batch minimum** — amortizes customs entry fees to <$1/unit
4. **Self-fulfill Phase 1** — 3PL minimums are too high for validation-stage volumes
5. **Use air freight for first batches** — comparable per-unit cost to sea at <100 units, 15-20 days faster
6. **Budget $7-8 landed cost per cotton T-shirt** — leaves healthy margin at $25-35 retail
