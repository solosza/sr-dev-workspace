## Go/No-Go Decision

### CONDITIONAL GO

The government contracting subcontract model is **legally viable, economically sound, and practically achievable** — but the pure "broker" model (win contract, subcontract 100%, pocket the spread) is **illegal**. The viable model requires the prime contractor to perform at least 50% of the work.

**Condition:** You must be willing and able to **perform at least 50% of the contract work yourself** (or subcontract to similarly situated small businesses that count toward your 50%). If the model depends on subcontracting everything to a single non-qualifying entity, it fails the FAR 52.219-14 test.

---

## What Specifically Kills the Pure Broker Model

**FAR 52.219-14 — Limitations on Subcontracting (the 50% Rule)**

For services contracts set aside for small business, the prime contractor cannot pay more than 50% of the contract amount to subcontractors that are not "similarly situated entities." This means:

- You **cannot** win a contract and hand 100% of the work to someone else
- You **must** perform at least 50% yourself (measured by cost of personnel)
- Exception: subcontracting to "similarly situated entities" (same small business status + small for the NAICS) counts toward YOUR 50%

**Ostensible Subcontractor Rule:** If a subcontractor performs the primary and vital requirements of the contract, the SBA may determine the sub — not you — is the actual contractor. This disqualifies the set-aside.

## What DOES Work — The Modified Model

### Model A: Prime Contractor + Technical Lead (Best for AI/Software)
1. Win contract as small business prime
2. **You perform 50%+** of the work: project management, technical architecture, AI/ML design, client communication, deliverable review, quality assurance
3. Subcontract specialized execution (coding, data engineering, infrastructure) to similarly situated small businesses
4. Your value-add: AI/kernel expertise, automation, delivery speed

**Why this works for AI consulting:** If you're providing the AI strategy, system design, and kernel-based automation — that IS the primary and vital work. The subcontractor handles execution under your direction.

### Model B: Mentor-Protege Joint Venture (Best for Larger Contracts)
1. Enter SBA Mentor-Protege program with an established contractor
2. Form a joint venture (qualifies as small business)
3. Mentor handles execution; you grow capability over 6 years
4. Past performance requirements are relaxed for proteges (2025 rule change)

### Model C: Subcontractor First (Best for Year 1)
1. Subcontract under an established prime contractor
2. Build federal past performance
3. Transition to prime contracting in Year 2-3
4. Lowest risk, lowest reward

## Recommended Contract Types

| Type | Recommendation | Why |
|------|---------------|-----|
| **Firm Fixed-Price (FFP)** | Primary target | Clear margin (bid price minus costs), no DCAA audit of costs |
| **Time & Materials (T&M)** | Secondary target | Preset hourly rates, spread = your rate minus sub rate |
| **IDIQ** | Growth vehicle | Win once, receive task orders over years; provides pipeline stability |
| **Cost-Plus** | Avoid initially | Low margins (7-8%), requires DCAA-compliant accounting, cost audits |

- [ ] Pursue IDIQ vehicles for pipeline stability
- [ ] Consider SBA certifications (8(a), HUBZone, etc.) for additional set-asides

- [ ] Target $500K-$1M contracts as past performance grows

## Conclusion

Government contracting is a viable and potentially lucrative revenue stream for an AI consulting LLC, but it is **not** a passive income model. The pure "bid and subcontract everything" broker model is illegal under FAR 52.219-14. The viable model requires genuine technical contribution (50%+ of work) with strategic subcontracting for specialized execution.

The strongest path: **use AI/kernel expertise as the 50%+ value-add** (architecture, design, automation, QA) while subcontracting execution-heavy tasks to similarly situated small businesses. This aligns perfectly with the Isagawa Kernel's capabilities — the kernel IS the competitive advantage.

**Decision: CONDITIONAL GO — proceed with LLC formation, SAM.gov registration, and opportunity scanning. First bid target: Q1 FY2027 (October-December 2026).**
