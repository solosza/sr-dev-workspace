# Practical Viability — Registration, Bonding, Risk, NAICS

## Research Date
2026-05-28

## Executive Summary

Practical barriers to entry are **manageable**. SAM.gov registration is free, bonding is generally **not required** for services contracts (only construction >$150K), and insurance costs are modest. The primary risks are prime contractor liability for subcontractor performance and cash flow during the 6-12 month ramp-up. NAICS codes for AI/software consulting are well-established with billions in annual federal spending.

## 1. Registration Requirements

### Step-by-Step Registration Path

| Step | Action | Timeline | Cost |
|------|--------|----------|------|
| 1 | Form LLC (Wyoming recommended — see 093 research) | 1-2 weeks | ~$100 |
| 2 | Obtain EIN from IRS | Immediate (online) | Free |
| 3 | Wait 14 days for EIN to propagate | 14 days | — |
| 4 | Register in SAM.gov (get UEI automatically) | 2-4 weeks | Free |
| 5 | Complete SAM.gov Representations & Certifications | During SAM registration | Free |
| 6 | Set up DCAA-compliant accounting (QuickBooks + proper chart of accounts) | 1-2 weeks | ~$30/mo |
| 7 | Obtain business insurance (see Section 4) | 1 week | Varies |
| 8 | Consider SBA certifications (8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, WOSB) | 1-6 months | Free |

### Beyond SAM.gov
- **State business license** — varies by state; Wyoming LLC operating in Hawaii needs Hawaii GET license
- **SAM.gov renewal** — annual renewal required; initiate 60 days before expiration
- **CMMC compliance** — required for DoD contracts handling CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information); may not apply to all IT services contracts
- **GSA Schedule** — optional but valuable; provides access to GSA's contract vehicle for easier ordering by agencies

### 2026 SAM.gov Changes
- Modernized FAR/DFARS Representations and Certifications released March 24, 2026
- Increased verification steps for renewals
- FAR Overhaul integration in progress

## 2. Bonding Requirements

### Services Contracts: Generally NOT Required

| Contract Type | Bonding Required? | Details |
|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Services (IT/consulting)** | **No** | Bonds are not typically required for services contracts |
| Construction >$150K | **Yes** | Performance + payment bonds required by Miller Act |
| Construction <$150K | No | Below threshold |
| Supplies | Rarely | Only if specified in solicitation |

### If Bonding IS Required
- **Bid bond:** Guarantees you'll accept the contract if awarded
- **Performance bond:** Guarantees you'll complete the work
- **Payment bond:** Guarantees you'll pay subcontractors
- **Cost:** 0.5-2% of contract value (premium) + 0.6% SBA fee if using SBA guarantee
- **SBA Surety Bond Guarantee Program:** Available for small businesses that can't get commercial bonds; covers up to $14M for federal contracts

### Bottom Line
For AI/software consulting services contracts, **bonding is not a barrier**. It's primarily a construction concern.

## 3. Timeline — Bid to Payment

### Full Lifecycle

| Phase | Duration | Cumulative |
|-------|----------|------------|
| SAM.gov registration | 2-6 weeks | 2-6 weeks |
| Opportunity identification | Ongoing | — |
| Proposal preparation | 2-4 weeks | 4-10 weeks |
| Government evaluation | 30-90 days | 3-6 months |
| Award notification | 1-2 weeks | 3-7 months |
| Contract kickoff | 1-2 weeks | 4-7 months |
| First work period + invoice | 30 days | 5-8 months |
| Payment (Prompt Payment Act) | **30 days** | **6-9 months** |

### Accelerators
- **Micro-purchases (<$15K):** Can be awarded via purchase card, minimal evaluation
- **Simplified acquisitions (<$350K):** Shorter evaluation periods
- **Emergency/urgent needs:** Expedited timelines
- **Q4 spending surge (July-September):** Agencies push contracts quickly to spend budgets

## 4. Risk — Subcontractor Non-Performance

### Prime Contractor Liability: ABSOLUTE

**You are 100% responsible for your subcontractor's work.** The government holds the prime contractor accountable for:
- Technical performance quality
- Delivery schedule compliance
- All compliance obligations
- Subcontractor payments (within 30 days)
- Flow-down clause compliance

**This liability cannot be transferred** through subcontract agreements. If your sub fails, the government comes after you.

### Consequences of Failure
- Contract termination for default
- Negative past performance evaluation (follows you for years)
- Potential suspension or debarment
- False Claims Act exposure if you misrepresented capabilities
- Financial liability for re-procurement costs

### Risk Mitigation Strategies
1. **Vet subcontractors thoroughly** — check past performance, references, financial stability
2. **Subcontract agreements** — clear deliverables, milestones, payment terms, termination clauses
3. **Require sub insurance** — general liability + professional liability (errors & omissions)
4. **Active management** — don't just hand off; review deliverables, manage milestones
5. **Performance bonds from subs** — optional but provides financial protection
6. **Start with small contracts** — limit exposure while building relationships with reliable subs

## 5. Insurance Requirements

### Recommended Coverage for IT/Software Consulting

| Insurance Type | Typical Requirement | Estimated Cost |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| General Liability | $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate | $500-$1,500/yr |
| Professional Liability (E&O) | $1M per occurrence | $1,000-$3,000/yr |
| Workers' Compensation | State-mandated if employees | Varies by state |
| Cyber Liability | $1M (increasingly required) | $1,000-$2,500/yr |
| Commercial Auto | If applicable | Varies |

### Contract-Specific Requirements
Each solicitation specifies insurance minimums. Common requirements:
- General liability: $500K-$1M
- Professional liability: $1M
- Workers' comp: As required by state law

### Total Estimated Insurance Cost
For a solo LLC or small team: **$2,500-$7,000/year**

## 6. NAICS Codes for AI/Software Consulting

### Primary Codes

| NAICS | Description | Federal Spending (2024) | Best For |
|-------|-------------|------------------------|----------|
| **541511** | Custom Computer Programming Services | $6.4B | Custom AI/ML applications, software development |
| **541512** | Computer Systems Design Services | $3.1B | AI/ML systems engineering, system integration |
| **541519** | Other Computer Related Services | $7.57B | Data science, analytics, IT consulting |
| **518210** | Computing Infrastructure Providers, Data Processing | — | Cloud services, data hosting |
| **541611** | Administrative Management & General Management Consulting | — | Business consulting with tech component |
| **541715** | Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences | — | AI research contracts |

### Recommended Strategy
Register for **multiple NAICS codes** in SAM.gov — most IT contractors qualify for 3-5 codes. This increases visibility to federal buyers.

**Primary codes to register:**
1. **541512** — Computer Systems Design (broadest, covers AI/ML)
2. **541511** — Custom Computer Programming (for development-heavy contracts)
3. **541519** — Other Computer Related Services (catch-all for analytics, consulting)

### Size Standards
Small business size standards for these NAICS codes are based on average annual receipts:
- 541511: $34M
- 541512: $34M
- 541519: $34M

A new LLC with <$34M in annual revenue easily qualifies as small business under these codes.
