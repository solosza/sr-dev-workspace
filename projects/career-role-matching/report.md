## Market Observations

### Demand Signals

1. **AI agent infrastructure is the hottest category.** 33 Tier 1 listings, most created in the last 60 days. Companies are building agent runtimes, oversight systems, and governance layers — exactly what the kernel does.

2. **"Agent governance" is emerging as a distinct role.** Geordie AI (RSAC 2026 winner), Vanta, and Rubric AI all treat agent governance as a product category, not just a feature. This is a new market forming in real time.

3. **Healthcare AI is well-funded and hiring aggressively.** Hippocratic AI has 76 open positions. Florida Blue and InductiveHealth are hiring senior architects. HIPAA compliance creates a moat — domain expertise is a differentiator.

4. **QA/SDET roles are shifting toward AI-native testing.** Secure Privacy explicitly uses Claude Code and Cursor. RapidFort needs pytest + Docker. The industry is moving from "automate clicks" to "build AI-powered test systems."

5. **Remote is available but not dominant.** 27/98 (28%) are remote. Remote roles cluster in Tier 2 (healthcare QA, enterprise SRE). Tier 1 AI agent startups tend to be on-site (SF/NYC).

### Key Trends

- **Vertical AI > horizontal AI** for role fit. Companies building AI for specific domains (compliance, healthcare, audit) value domain expertise more than pure ML skills.
- **Agent oversight is the new DevOps.** Just as DevOps emerged from the need to govern deployment pipelines, agent oversight is emerging from the need to govern AI agent behavior.
- **Compliance expertise compounds.** HIPAA + SOC 2 + STIG + CIS across the profile creates a compliance portfolio that few candidates can match.
- **Solo builders are valued at startups.** Founding roles (Rubric, PunttAI, Moby) explicitly want people who can ship alone. 37 repos across 2 orgs is strong evidence.
