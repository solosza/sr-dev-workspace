# Portfolio Message Research Report

## Validation Verdict

Message validated as differentiated.

**CONFIRMED DIFFERENTIATED** — The solo-founder-velocity thesis with cryptographic attestation is genuinely unique in the current market. No competitor, peer portfolio, or funded team leads with Sigstore-backed proof of autonomous pipeline output. The message stands.

## Market Landscape

### How AI Engineers Position Themselves (Query 1)
- **Skills-list portfolios dominate.** Most AI/ML engineer portfolios lead with technology stacks (PyTorch, LangChain, RAG pipelines) and project galleries. Few lead with a thesis or velocity claim.
- **"Agent builder" is overcrowded.** Every second portfolio now says "I build AI agents." The phrase has no signal value left.
- **Nobody shows provenance.** Not a single portfolio found that surfaces cryptographic proof of work completion. Portfolios assert competence; none evidence it with a verifiable chain.
- **Demo-first portfolios exist but are rare.** A handful of engineers embed live demos (chatbots, code generators), but these prove the artifact works, not that the builder ships at velocity.

### What the Market Wants (Query 2 — FDE/Applied AI JDs)
- **Autonomy and velocity are top signals.** Anthropic FDE postings emphasize "work autonomously," "ship fast," and "own the full stack from prototype to production." These are the exact qualities the solo-founder-velocity thesis claims.
- **Proof of shipping > proof of knowing.** JDs increasingly ask for "portfolio of shipped products" or "evidence of end-to-end delivery," not just credentials or papers.
- **Governance and observability are emerging.** Multiple JDs reference "responsible AI," "audit trails," and "production observability" — the exact category cryptographic attestation falls into.

### How Funded Teams Position (Query 3)
- **Cognition (Devin):** Leads with "autonomous software engineer" — replaces junior developers. Their claim: Devin writes code without human intervention. Weakness: they frame AI as a replacement, not a multiplier.
- **Factory.ai:** Positions as "AI-powered development teams." Their claim: team-scale output. Weakness: they're selling a product, not demonstrating personal capability.
- **What only a solo founder can claim:** "I produce at team velocity, and every run is signed." Funded teams can't claim per-person velocity because they have teams. A solo founder with 80+ attested pipeline runs IS the proof that one person can match a small team — and the attestation chain makes it unfalsifiable.

## The Gap

**Nobody leads with verifiable output velocity as their primary positioning.**

The market has:
- Skills lists (generic, no signal)
- Project galleries (assertion without evidence)
- Live demos (proves artifact, not builder velocity)
- Funded team claims (team output, not per-person)

The market does NOT have:
- A portfolio that says "here are 80+ cryptographically signed pipeline runs proving one person shipped this"
- Sigstore/Rekor as a portfolio differentiator (the technology exists for supply chain security, but nobody has applied it to personal portfolio credibility)
- A framing where the proof IS the product — where the attestation chain is front-and-center, not buried in a footer

## Cryptographic Provenance (Query 4)

- **Sigstore is established in supply chain security** but has zero presence in portfolio/personal-brand contexts. It's used by npm, PyPI, Kubernetes — never by an individual to prove shipping velocity.
- **"AI attestation" is not yet a category.** Searches for this term return academic papers on model provenance and a few governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF), not portfolio applications.
- **Emerging but unowned:** Google's A2A protocol and AgentHook are building toward agent-to-agent provenance, but these are infrastructure plays. Nobody owns the narrative of "cryptographic proof that a human directed an AI pipeline to produce real output."
- **First-mover advantage is real and time-limited.** Once Sigstore-based attestation becomes standard for AI agent output (likely 12-18 months), the differentiation shifts from "I do this" to "I did this first." The portfolio should claim the position now.

## What Resonates (HN Signal — Query 5)

- **Demos beat credentials, every time.** "Show HN" posts with live, interactive demos consistently outperform posts that describe capabilities without showing them.
- **Numbers beat narrative.** Posts that lead with concrete metrics ("processes 10K documents in 2 minutes," "deployed to 50 customers") get more engagement than posts with philosophical framing.
- **Solo-founder stories get outsized attention.** HN has a strong bias toward individual makers. "I built X alone" triggers curiosity; "our team built X" does not.
- **Transparency is credibility.** Posts that show the messy process (logs, errors, iterations) are received as more authentic than polished marketing pages. The feed's raw pipeline output IS this transparency.

## Refined Message

The original thesis holds without modification. The framing should be:

**Core claim:** One person, team-scale output, every run cryptographically attested.

**What to say on story.html:**
- Lead with the loop mechanic (sentence in → attested output out), not with "who I am"
- Let the numbers do the asserting — don't claim velocity, show it
- Frame against funded teams implicitly: "they replace developers; this multiplies them"
- Make the attestation chain visible, not explained — show the Rekor links, don't write paragraphs about what Sigstore is
- The feed entries (including messy/failed ones) are credibility, not a flaw

**What NOT to say:**
- Don't say "agent factory" (generic, every portfolio says it)
- Don't say "vibe coder" (diminishes the engineering rigor)
- Don't lead with skills or technologies (noise)
- Don't explain Sigstore in detail on the landing page (link to attestation page for depth)

## Copy Elements to Use

- **Hero headline:** "One founder. Team-scale output. Every run signed."
- **Loop mechanic one-liner:** "A sentence becomes a backlog item. The pipeline decomposes, executes, and signs. No human between start and finish."
- **Numbers to lead with:** `80+` cryptographically attested runs, `800+` tasks executed, `9` production repos — all verifiable on Rekor
- **Differentiator line:** "Most portfolios assert competence. This one evidences it cryptographically."
- **Anti-Devin framing:** "They replace juniors. I multiply seniors." (use sparingly — works as a one-liner, not a thesis)
- **Authenticity hook:** "The messy runs are still in the feed. That's the point."
