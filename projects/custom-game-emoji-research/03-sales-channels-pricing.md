## Revenue Share Detail

### itch.io
- **Creator controls the split**: default 10%, adjustable 0-100%
- Payment processing (Stripe/PayPal): ~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (always deducted)
- **Net to creator on $5 sale** (10% share): $5.00 - $0.50 (itch) - $0.45 (processing) = **$4.05 (81%)**
- **Net to creator on $5 sale** (0% share): $5.00 - $0.00 - $0.45 = **$4.55 (91%)**
- Creator Day: itch.io takes 0% for 24 hours (annual event, massive sales spike)

### Gumroad
- **Flat 10% + $0.50 per direct sale** (all sellers, no volume discounts)
- Gumroad Discover: **30% flat** on sales sourced through Discover marketplace
- Payment processing: ~2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe) on top of Gumroad's cut
- **Net to creator on $5 direct sale**: $5.00 - $0.50 (10%) - $0.50 (flat) - $0.45 (processing) = **$3.55 (71%)**
- Gumroad acts as Merchant of Record (handles VAT/GST globally since Jan 2025)

### Direct Licensing
- No platform fees — only payment processing (~3%)
- Common models: per-project license ($50-500), per-studio license ($200-2000), exclusive license (negotiated)
- Requires own storefront/portfolio + outreach
- Higher margins but lower volume

## Bundle Revenue

### itch.io Bundles
- Creators submit assets to community bundles (e.g., Ultimate Game Asset Bundle)
- Revenue split equally among all participants (unless stated otherwise pre-publication)
- Exposure to bundle buyers — high volume, low per-unit revenue
- Good for visibility/marketing; less effective for direct revenue

### Humble Bundle
- Primarily for games, not standalone asset packs
- Compensation: upfront payments, revenue share, or hybrid
- Three-way split: Humble, developer/publisher, charity
- Massive exposure (millions of potential customers)
- Best for: established creators with catalog depth

## Subscription Model Viability

### Existing Subscription Icon Services (Non-Game-Specific)
| Service | Price | Assets | Model |
|---------|-------|--------|-------|
| IconScout | $12/month | 9M+ icons, illustrations, 3D | Unlimited downloads |
| Icons8 | ~$10/month | Icons, illustrations, photos | Tiered plans |
| Creative Fabrica | $3.99/month | Fonts, graphics, crafts | Full library access |

### Game Asset Subscription Precedent
- **No established subscription model** for indie game icon/sprite packs specifically
- Asset packs on itch.io/Gumroad are overwhelmingly one-time purchase
- Some creators use **Patreon** for monthly content drops (sprites, tilesets, icons) — typically $5-15/month tiers
- **Game-specific subscription** would need: regular new content, exclusive access, engine-specific formats

### Viability Assessment
- **Low probability for pure subscription** at launch — indie devs expect one-time purchases
- **Hybrid model possible:** one-time pack purchase + optional Patreon/subscription for monthly additions
- Patreon model works for prolific pixel artists (e.g., 50-100 new icons/month as subscriber perk)
- Would require consistent release cadence to justify recurring cost

## Key Takeaways

1. **itch.io is the primary channel** — lowest fees, largest indie game dev audience, best discoverability for game assets
2. **Gumroad as secondary** — higher fees but good for email-list-driven sales and broader audience
3. **Direct licensing is bonus revenue** — high margin but requires sales effort
4. **Pricing sweet spot:** $5-15 for 100-500 icon packs; $15-25 for 500+ mega packs
5. **No competitor exists** in grid-tactical emoji specifically — first-mover advantage in a small but underserved niche
6. **Subscription unlikely at launch** — one-time purchase with optional Patreon for updates is more viable
7. **Realistic Year 1 revenue: $500-5,000** depending on marketing effort and niche capture
8. **Revenue scales with catalog** — multiple themed packs + updates compound over time
