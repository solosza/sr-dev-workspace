## 5. Manual Tester Monetization

Open source platform = free. Knowledge = paid.

| Tier | What | Price | Scale |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| **Free** | Open source platform, YouTube tutorials, blog posts | $0 | Unlimited — builds audience |
| **Self-paced course** | "Manual to Automation in 30 Days" — structured curriculum, exercises | $199-$499 | Infinite scale, record once |
| **Live cohort** | 4-week group training, weekly live sessions, hands-on with their site | $997-$1,997 | 10-20 per cohort |
| **1:1 coaching** | Private sessions, build on their actual app | $150-$250/hr | Doesn't scale, but highest per-unit |
| **Certification** | "Isagawa Certified QA Automation Engineer" — badge, exam, portfolio | $299 | Adds to course upsell |

Same model as Docker, Terraform, Kubernetes — tool is free, training/certification is the business.

## 9. Competitive Positioning Summary

### Four Quadrant Framework

- **Proprietary platforms** (mabl, Virtuoso): enforce standards but lock you in
- **AI generators** (ChatGPT, Copilot): fast but messy, no standards
- **Open source** (Serenity BDD): standards but no AI
- **Isagawa**: AI speed + Open source + Enforced standards (unique position)

### Key Distinction — AI Governance vs AI Execution Management

| AI Governance (Others) | AI Execution Management (Isagawa) |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Monitors AI behavior | Controls AI behavior |
| Documents compliance | Enforces compliance |
| Alerts on violations | Prevents violations |
| Audits after execution | Gates during execution |
| "Did the AI do it right?" | "The AI can only do it right" |
