# Alternatives — SBA Loans, ROBS, Bootstrapping

## Research Date
2026-05-28

## Executive Summary

For a solo AI/software consulting LLC, **bootstrapping is the superior strategy** — startup costs are under $1K, revenue can start immediately, and 78% of small businesses self-fund successfully. Credit stacking adds unnecessary risk and debt for a business that doesn't need capital-intensive equipment or inventory. SBA loans and ROBS are available but overkill for a consulting startup.

---

## 1. SBA 7(a) Loans

### Program Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Maximum loan | **$5 million** (up to $10M combined with 504) |
| Interest rate | Prime + 2.25-4.75% (currently ~10-13%) |
| Terms | Up to 25 years (real estate), 10 years (equipment), 7 years (working capital) |
| Approval timeline | **60-90 days** (faster with Preferred Lenders) |
| Equity injection | 10% minimum for startups |
| Collateral | Required for loans over $500K |

### New Business Requirements
- Sound business plan with financial projections
- Good personal credit (no minimum SBSS score required since March 2026)
- 10% equity injection (you put in $1 for every $9 the SBA backs)
- Industry experience (not always required but strongly preferred)
- Clear loan purpose (working capital, equipment, real estate)

### Pros vs. Credit Stacking
- **Lower interest rate** (10-13% vs. 17-25% on credit cards)
- **Longer terms** (7-10 years vs. 12-18 months at 0%)
- **No personal guarantee on first $25K** (for some loan types)
- **Business development support** from SBA

### Cons vs. Credit Stacking
- **60-90 day approval** (credit cards are instant)
- **Requires business plan, projections** (credit cards don't)
- **10% equity injection** (you need money to get money)
- **Limited to business expenses** (not for owner salary draws)
- **Collateral may be required** for larger loans

### Bottom Line
SBA 7(a) loans are better for **established businesses** needing working capital or equipment. For a brand-new consulting LLC with no revenue, the approval odds are low and the timeline is long.

## 2. SBA Microloans

### Program Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Maximum loan | **$50,000** (average: ~$13,000) |
| Interest rate | 8-13% |
| Terms | Up to 7 years |
| Application through | SBA-approved intermediary lenders |
| Available to startups | **Yes** |

### Pros vs. Credit Stacking
- Lower interest (8-13% vs. 17-25%)
- Includes mentorship and business guidance
- Available to startups with no revenue
- Builds SBA lending relationship

### Cons vs. Credit Stacking
- **Max $50K** — not enough for the $250K target
- Takes 2-8 weeks for approval
- Requires business plan
- Can't use for paying yourself directly
- Limited intermediary availability by geography

## 3. ROBS (Rollover for Business Startups)

### How It Works
1. Create a **C-Corporation** (not LLC — this is a requirement)
2. Establish a 401(k) plan for the corporation
3. Roll your existing retirement funds into the new 401(k)
4. The 401(k) buys stock in your corporation
5. The corporation uses the stock purchase price as operating capital
6. **No early withdrawal penalties or taxes** on the rollover

### Requirements
- Existing 401(k) or IRA with funds to roll over
- Must form a C-Corp (not LLC)
- Must hire a ROBS provider for compliance (~$2K-$5K setup)
- Must offer 401(k) to all eligible employees
- Annual Form 5500 filing required
- Owner must pay themselves a "reasonable salary"

### IRS Scrutiny — HIGH
The IRS actively investigates ROBS arrangements:
- **50% of ROBS businesses fail within 5 years**
- Common audit triggers: missing Form 5500, not offering plan to employees, improper valuations
- IRS has a dedicated **ROBS Compliance Project**
- "Results indicated that most ROBS businesses either failed or were on the road to failure"
- Can result in plan disqualification, back taxes, and penalties

### Pros
- Tax-free access to retirement funds (potentially $100K+)
- No debt — you're investing in your own company
- No interest payments

### Cons
- **Extremely high risk** — 50% failure rate, IRS scrutiny
- Requires C-Corp structure (not LLC)
- $2K-$5K setup cost plus ongoing compliance
- **You risk your retirement savings** — if business fails, retirement is gone
- Complex ongoing compliance requirements

### Bottom Line
ROBS is **the nuclear option**. Only consider if you have substantial retirement savings AND are confident in the business model. For a consulting LLC, the risk/reward ratio is terrible — you're betting your retirement on something that costs almost nothing to start.

## 4. Bootstrapping

### Why It Works for AI/Software Consulting

| Cost Category | Amount |
|--------------|--------|
| LLC formation (Wyoming) | $100 |
| Registered agent | $100/year |
| Business insurance | $2,500-$7,000/year |
| Website/domain | $100-$500/year |
| Software tools (Claude Code, etc.) | $200-$500/month |
| Computer/equipment | Already owned |
| Office space | Home office (deductible) |
| **Total startup cost** | **< $1,000** |
| **Annual operating cost** | **$5,000-$15,000** |

### Revenue Timeline for Consulting
- **Week 1:** LLC formed, start reaching out to network
- **Month 1-2:** First consulting engagement ($5K-$15K)
- **Month 3-6:** 2-3 active clients ($10K-$30K/month)
- **Month 6-12:** Steady pipeline ($15K-$50K/month)

### Statistics
- **78% of small businesses** use personal savings to start (U.S. Chamber)
- **60% of bootstrapped startups** reach profitability within 3 years
- Only **35% of externally funded startups** reach profitability in same period

### Bootstrapping for the Isagawa Model
The specific situation here is ideal for bootstrapping:
- **Products already built** (kernel, QA platform, SSH compliance, vibe coder)
- **Skills are the product** — no inventory, no manufacturing, no capital equipment
- **Revenue starts immediately** — consulting can begin day one
- **AI leverage** — Isagawa Kernel lets you deliver at 3-5x speed, meaning higher effective hourly rate

## 6. Verdict: Best Funding Strategy for AI Consulting LLC

### Recommendation: **Bootstrap + Strategic Credit**

| Strategy | When | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| **Bootstrap first** | Day 1 | Costs <$1K, no debt, revenue can start immediately |
| **2-3 business credit cards** | Month 1 | Build business credit, 0% intro for short-term cash flow smoothing, rewards |
| **SBA microloan** | Month 6+ | If specific capital need arises (e.g., hiring, equipment) |
| **Business LOC** | Year 1+ | Once revenue established, get LOC for working capital flexibility |

### What NOT to Do
- **Don't stack $250K in credit** unless you have a capital-intensive business (manufacturing, retail, real estate)
- **Don't use ROBS** — the risk/compliance burden isn't worth it for consulting
- **Don't take an SBA 7(a)** — consulting doesn't need that much capital

### The Simple Truth
AI/software consulting is a **low-capital, high-margin** business. The @hxxntrr strategy is designed for businesses that need **upfront capital to build something before revenue starts** (hardware, inventory, physical product). A consulting LLC's "product" is the founder's brain — you don't need $250K to start selling that.
