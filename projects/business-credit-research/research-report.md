## Decision: NO-GO for Credit Stacking as Primary Funding Strategy

### Recommendation: BOOTSTRAP

Credit stacking is **legal and technically feasible** — a 750+ FICO can realistically access $75K-$150K in 0% APR business credit. However, **it is the wrong tool for an AI consulting LLC**. Consulting is a low-capital, high-margin business that doesn't need $150K+ in startup capital. The risk (personal guarantee on six figures of debt) dramatically exceeds the benefit (funding you don't actually need).

---

## Why Credit Stacking Is Wrong for This Business

### The Core Problem

The @hxxntrr claim: "Take $250K from banks, pay yourself $7K/month for 3 years."

**The math breaks after the intro period:**

| Phase | Monthly Burn | What Happens |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Months 1-12 (0% APR) | $7K salary + $2.5K minimums = $9.5K | Works — burning $9.5K/month of credit |
| Month 12 | ~$114K used, ~$36K remaining | Intro period ends |
| Months 13+ (20% APR) | $7K salary + $4K+ interest + $5K+ minimums | **Death spiral** — paying interest with borrowed money |

**The $7K salary model only works if:**
1. You stack the full $250K (high end, not guaranteed)
2. Business generates revenue before month 12
3. You aggressively pay down before rates jump

**If the business generates revenue by month 12, you didn't need the credit in the first place.**

### The Real Cost

| Scenario | Total Interest Over 3 Years | Total Payments |
|----------|---------------------------|----------------|
| $150K stack, pay off in 3 years | ~$50,000 | ~$200,000 |
| $250K stack, pay off in 3 years | ~$83,000 | ~$333,000 |

You'd pay $50K-$83K in interest for the privilege of borrowing money you didn't need.

### What You're Actually Risking

- **Personal guarantee** on $150K+ — your personal assets are on the line
- **Credit score destruction** if anything goes wrong (500-550 for 7-10 years)
- **Potential personal bankruptcy** — Chapter 7 or 13
- **Psychological burden** — six figures of high-interest debt while building a business

## When Credit Stacking WOULD Make Sense

Credit stacking is appropriate for:
- **Retail businesses** needing inventory ($50K-$200K upfront)
- **Construction/trades** needing equipment
- **SaaS companies** needing to hire a team before revenue
- **Franchise purchases** ($100K-$500K buy-in)

It is NOT appropriate for:
- Solo consulting (no capital needed)
- Service businesses (skills are the product)
- Side businesses transitioning to full-time (revenue exists before quitting)
