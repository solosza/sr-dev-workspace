# Positioning Report — Isagawa Homepage

## Positioning Alternative 1: Loop Engineering Framework

**Angle:** Architecture focus
**Headline:** "The enforcement loop for AI agents."
**Best for:** AI infrastructure engineers, framework builders
**Verdict:** Strong differentiation but too abstract for lead positioning. Better as a supporting explanation in Section 01.

## Positioning Alternative 2: Governance + Enforcement Platform

**Angle:** Operational focus
**Headline:** "Governance that agents can't bypass."
**Best for:** Compliance teams, QA leads, risk-conscious CTOs
**Verdict:** Clearest value proposition. "Can't bypass" is immediately understandable and mechanically demonstrable. Should lead the homepage.

## Positioning Alternative 3: Self-Improving Agent Architecture

**Angle:** Innovation focus
**Headline:** "The agent framework that builds itself."
**Best for:** AI researchers, innovation-focused founders
**Verdict:** Most differentiated in the market — no competitor claims self-improvement. Should be the supporting narrative that deepens after the lead.

## Positioning Alternative 4: Compliance Automation Without the Limits

**Angle:** Use case focus
**Headline:** "Compliance-grade AI agents, built in minutes."
**Best for:** Compliance automation teams, regulated industries
**Verdict:** Concrete but narrows the market. Better suited for a dedicated landing page, not the main homepage.

## Recommended Positioning

**Lead with enforcement (Alt 2), deepen with self-improvement (Alt 3).**

The homepage should communicate two things in sequence:
1. **What it does:** Agents governed by rules they physically can't bypass
2. **What makes it unique:** The governance itself improves from every failure

This positions Isagawa in unoccupied territory: not a platform for building agents (LangChain, CrewAI) and not observability for agents (W&B) — but a framework that makes agents trustworthy by design.
