# Hero Copy — Variant B: Business Value

## Hero Headline
AI agents that follow the rules. Every time.

## Hero Subheadline
Isagawa turns AI coding agents into reliable operators — governed, auditable, and improving with every task.

## Hero Description
AI agents drift. They skip quality checks, ignore instructions after thousands of tokens, and repeat the same mistakes across sessions. Isagawa solves this with mechanical enforcement — not guidelines agents can ignore, but gates they physically cannot bypass. Every action is checked. Every failure becomes a permanent lesson. Every output is cryptographically signed. The result: agents you can trust to operate consistently, session after session, at scale.
