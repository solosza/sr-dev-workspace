### Notes on Meta AI

Meta's career page is less granular than Anthropic/OpenAI for agent-specific roles. Meta's AI investment is heavily focused on:
- **Llama model development** (open-source LLMs)
- **MTIA custom silicon** (AI hardware)
- **Muse Spark** (multi-agent orchestration for Meta AI)
- **AI Infrastructure** at massive scale (distributed systems, compilers, optimization)

Meta has fewer explicitly "agent infrastructure" roles compared to Anthropic/OpenAI/Google. Their AI work is more integrated into product teams (Instagram, WhatsApp, Horizon) rather than standalone agent platform teams. The strongest matches are infrastructure roles that support generative AI systems.

### Key Observations

- **Google has the richest agent-specific job market** — 14 strong matches across DeepMind, Cloud AI, and Applied AI teams
- **Google's "Agent Protocol" role** is a near-perfect fit for the Isagawa Kernel value proposition (protocol-driven agent governance)
- **xAI is investing heavily in coding agents** — 4 dedicated "Coding Agents" team roles covering infra, RL, evals, and product
- **Meta has fewer agent-specific roles** — their AI investment is more focused on model training (Llama) and infrastructure (MTIA silicon) than agent platform engineering
- **No remote options** in this batch — all three companies require relocation to Bay Area (Google: Sunnyvale/Mountain View, Meta: Menlo Park, xAI: Palo Alto/SF)
- **Senior/Staff level roles dominate** at Google — matches well with 20+ years experience
- **xAI's "Member of Technical Staff" titles** are flat-hierarchy; equivalent to senior/staff at larger companies
- **Google DeepMind safety roles** (Frontier Safety, Responsibility Engineering, Autonomous Security) are strong fits for the governance/enforcement angle
