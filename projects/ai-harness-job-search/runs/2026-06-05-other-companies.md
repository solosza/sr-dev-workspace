### Key Observations

- **Scale AI dominates agent infrastructure hiring** — 11 strong matches, the most of any company in this search. Their "Frontier Agents" team is purpose-built for agent systems at enterprise scale.
- **LangChain is the most mission-aligned company** — they literally build the agent orchestration and evaluation stack (LangChain, LangGraph, LangSmith). 7 strong matches.
- **Letta is the purest agent startup** — small team, 100% focused on agent infrastructure. High-risk/high-reward startup environment.
- **Cohere has rebranded around "Agentic Platform"** — strong signal of strategic commitment to agent infrastructure.
- **Databricks' Agent Quality role is uniquely relevant** — founding an agent evaluation team, directly aligned with governance/evaluation experience.
- **Mistral and Hugging Face have fewer agent-specific roles** — Mistral focuses on model training; Hugging Face on open-source ML tools.
- **Modal and W&B are adjacent** — they serve the AI agent ecosystem but don't build agent infrastructure themselves.
- **Most roles require Bay Area relocation** — SF, NYC dominate. Remote options exist at Cohere, Hugging Face, and W&B.
- **"Agent evaluation" is the hottest category** — Scale AI, LangChain, and Databricks all have dedicated eval infrastructure teams.
