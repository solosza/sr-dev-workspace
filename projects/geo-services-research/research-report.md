## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| **AI search algorithms change** | Medium | Techniques are foundational (structured data, authority) — not gaming-dependent |
| **Defensibility window closes (12-24 months)** | Medium | Use window to build client base + referral network; add advanced services |
| **Hawaii market too small** | Low | Tourism sector expands market; can serve mainland clients remotely |
| **Client churn** | Medium | Show measurable results within 4-8 weeks; tie to lead tracking |
| **Distraction from core business** | Medium | Cap at 5-8 clients; systemize delivery; consider VA for admin |

### The Defensibility Question
GEO techniques will become mainstream within 1-2 years. But:
- **First-mover advantage in Hawaii** — no one is doing walk-in GEO demos here
- **Client switching cost** — once you're their GEO provider and delivering results, they don't switch
- **Expertise deepens** — you'll learn what actually works (not what blog posts say)
- **Referral network compounds** — early clients generate later clients
