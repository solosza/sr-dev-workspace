## Sales Process

### The Walk-In Demo (Austin Model)
1. Walk into firm's lobby
2. Ask to speak with office manager or marketing contact
3. Open Perplexity on phone: "Search for [their firm name]"
4. Show them they don't appear (most won't)
5. Search for a competitor who does appear
6. "I can fix this. Here's what it costs."

**Why this works:**
- Tangible, immediate proof of the problem
- Competitor comparison creates urgency
- No abstract pitch — they see it with their own eyes
- Low-tech, high-impact demo

### Alternative Sales Channels
| Channel | Effort | Conversion | Scale |

## Competition Assessment

### Current GEO Agency Landscape
The GEO services market is emerging but growing fast:
- **Established agencies:** WebFX, Disruptive Advertising, Digital Elevator — large agencies adding GEO to existing SEO services
- **GEO-native startups:** RevvGrowth, Geoptie, LLMrefs — focused exclusively on AI search
- **Solo consultants:** Growing number of individuals (like the Austin teenager) selling GEO directly

### Competitive Advantage for a Solo Operator
- **Speed:** Can start today with minimal overhead
- **Local focus:** Big agencies don't do walk-in demos
- **AI expertise:** Technical understanding of how LLMs work (stronger pitch than most SEO consultants)
- **Pricing:** Can undercut agencies significantly ($2K vs. $5K+/month)
