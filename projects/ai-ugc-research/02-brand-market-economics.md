## Are Brands Actually Buying AI UGC?

### The Evidence: Yes, But With Caveats

**What's happening:**
- Brands that cannot source enough organic video UGC are turning to AI UGC tools to fill the gap
- AI UGC is being used primarily for **paid ad creative** (Meta ads, TikTok ads), not organic brand channels
- The use case is volume: brands need 10-50+ creative variations per campaign for A/B testing
- AI UGC platforms like Coinis generate clips from a product URL and launch them directly as Meta ads with ROAS tracking

**What's NOT happening:**
- Brands are NOT replacing their entire creator strategy with AI
- AI UGC is supplementing human UGC, not replacing it
- Premium brands still prefer authentic human creators for brand storytelling
- The market is splitting: AI for performance/volume, humans for authenticity/brand building

### Market Size
- UGC market forecast: $7.1B (2025) → $32.6B (2030), growing 29.4% annually
- AI UGC is a subset of this — no standalone market sizing yet, but growing fastest

## Go-to-Market: How AI UGC Creators Find Brand Deals

### Channel 1: Direct Outreach
- Cold DM/email brands with portfolio of AI-generated samples
- Show before/after: "$2 compute vs. $500 creator fee for equivalent output"
- Target: DTC brands, e-commerce, travel companies with high content volume needs

- Position as "AI content agency" offering volume production

- This is the most scalable model for a solo operator

## Unit Economics

### Cost Per Video
| Method | Cost per 30-sec Video | Time to Produce |
|--------|----------------------|-----------------|
| AI (Seedance 2.0 Fast) | $0.77-$2.00 | 8-15 minutes |
| AI (Seedance 2.0 Standard) | $1.21-$3.00 | 15-30 minutes |
| Beginner human creator | $75-$300 | 1-3 days |
| Mid-tier human creator | $300-$700 | 1-5 days |
| Premium human creator | $800-$2,000 | 3-7 days |

**The 99x cost gap:** A mid-tier creator charges $400-$700 for a single 30-second video. AI UGC tools generate the equivalent for $3.52-$4.90. That's a **99x cost reduction per unit**.

## Solo vs. Team Operation

### Solo Operator (Viable)
- **What you do:** Prompt engineering, client management, quality review, delivery
- **Tools needed:** Seedance subscription ($16-$44/mo), video editor, client management
- **Capacity:** 50-100 videos/month (limited by client acquisition, not production)
- **Revenue ceiling:** $5K-$15K/month solo

### Team (Scale Play)
- **When to hire:** After hitting $10K+/month consistently
- **First hire:** Sales/account manager ($3K-$5K/month)
- **Second hire:** Additional prompt engineer ($2K-$4K/month)
- **Revenue ceiling:** $50K-$100K+/month with team

## Target Verticals

| Vertical | Why AI UGC Works | Volume Need |
|----------|-----------------|------------|
| **Travel/Tourism** | Scenery-heavy, no human face needed | High — destinations need constant fresh content |
| **DTC E-commerce** | Product demos, lifestyle shots | Very high — 10-50 variations per product |
| **Real Estate** | Property walkthroughs, neighborhood tours | Medium — per-listing content |
| **Food & Beverage** | Recipe videos, product showcases | High — seasonal campaigns |
| **Tech/SaaS** | Product demos, feature highlights | Medium — per-release |
| **Fitness** | Workout demos, supplement showcases | High — routine variety |

**Best entry point:** Travel + DTC e-commerce. Both need high volume, tolerate AI content, and have clear ROI metrics.

## Key Takeaway

The unit economics are exceptional (95%+ gross margin). Brands ARE buying AI UGC, primarily for paid ad creative where volume and speed matter more than authenticity. The go-to-market is direct outreach + agency model. Solo operation is viable at $5K-$15K/month.
