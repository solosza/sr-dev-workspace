# Research Economic Viability of Govcon Subcontract Model

## Context
Research the economics of government contracting as a small business: typical margins, contract types that favor the broker model, volume of opportunities, and competition levels.

## Requirements
Answer these questions:
1. Typical margins: What spread can you capture between government price and sub cost?
2. Contract types: Which types (fixed-price, T&M, IDIQ) favor the broker model?
3. Volume: How many sub-$250K contracts are posted monthly on SAM.gov?
4. Competition: How many bidders typically compete for small set-aside contracts?
5. Payment timeline: How long from award to first payment?
6. What are realistic revenue expectations for year 1?
