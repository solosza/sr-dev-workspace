# Task 001: Research Existing Mod Managers

## Type
RESEARCH

## Objective
Comprehensive landscape analysis of existing mod management tools. What's already out there, what do they do well, what are their gaps.

## Steps
1. Research major mod managers:
   - **Vortex** (Nexus Mods official) — games supported, features, limitations
   - **Mod Organizer 2 (MO2)** — virtual filesystem approach, Bethesda focus
   - **r2modman / Thunderstore** — newer, lighter weight
   - **Steam Workshop** — built-in, limitations
   - **SMAPI** (Stardew Valley specific)
   - **Wabbajack** — modlist installer (curated packs)
   - **CurseForge app** — Minecraft focus
2. For each tool, document:
   - Games supported
   - Install method (manual, auto-detect, one-click)
   - Conflict resolution approach
   - Load order management
   - UI vs CLI
   - Free vs paid
   - Community size / adoption
3. Identify what NONE of them do well:
   - Cross-game universal management?
   - AI-assisted mod discovery/recommendation?
   - Automated downloading from multiple sites?
   - Game-specific setup guidance?
4. Document the FM24 modding ecosystem specifically — why is it underserved?

## Deliverable
`projects/universal-mod-installer-research/01-existing-mod-managers.md`

## Acceptance Criteria
- At least 6 mod managers analyzed with feature comparison table
- Gap analysis identifying what existing tools miss
- FM24 modding ecosystem assessment
