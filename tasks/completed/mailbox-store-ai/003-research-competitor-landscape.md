# 003 — Research Competitor Landscape

## Type
RESEARCH

## Action
Research existing solutions that serve mailbox stores and pack-and-ship businesses. Identify:

1. **Existing software** — PostalMate, PackageLog, Corelytics, ShipStation, Pirate Ship, etc.
2. **What they do well** — which pain points do they solve?
3. **What they DON'T do** — gaps in current solutions (no AI, no proactive notifications, no cross-carrier optimization)
4. **AI-specific competitors** — any AI tools targeting small retail/service businesses?
5. **Pricing** — what do current solutions charge? (monthly, per-transaction, per-location)
6. **Market size** — how many mailbox stores exist in the US? (UPS Store ~5,300, PostNet ~700, independent stores)

## Web Research Queries
- "mailbox store management software"
- "pack and ship store POS"
- "PostalMate vs PackageLog"
- "AI for small business operations"
- "UPS Store franchise count" / "mailbox store market size"

## Target File
`docs/research/mailbox-store-ai-competitors.md`

## Acceptance
- [ ] At least 5 existing solutions listed with pricing
- [ ] Gap analysis: what current tools miss
- [ ] Market size estimate (number of stores)
- [ ] At least 1 AI competitor or "no AI competitors found"
