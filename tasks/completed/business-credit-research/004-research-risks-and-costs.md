# Research Risks and Costs — Personal Guarantee, Default, True Cost

## Context

## Requirements
Answer these questions:
1. Personal guarantee: Do business credit cards require personal guarantee? What happens on default?
2. Default consequences: What happens if the business fails and can't pay? Bankruptcy implications?
3. True cost: If you carry $250K at typical business card APRs, what's the monthly interest? Total cost over 3 years?
4. Minimum payments: What are typical minimum payment requirements? Can you service $250K in minimums from a $7K/month salary?
5. Credit score impact: How does $250K in business credit utilization affect personal credit?
6. Legal/tax implications: Is using business credit for salary a legitimate business expense? Any IRS concerns?
7. What distinguishes this from fraud? Where's the legal line?
