# Task 004: Research Curiosity Harness Product Concept

**Type:** research
**Action:** Analyze "quality questions" as product, market opportunity, differentiation

## Research Questions
- Does a market exist for teaching people to ask better questions of AI?
- What does a "curiosity harness" look like concretely?

## Acceptance Criteria
- Market analysis with existing competitors/adjacent products
- Product concept sketched (what it is, who it's for)
- Clear differentiation from prompt engineering
- Kernel skill vs standalone assessment
