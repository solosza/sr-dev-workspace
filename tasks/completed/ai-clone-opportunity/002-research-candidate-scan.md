# Research Candidate Scan

## Type
RESEARCH

## Description
Identify 10-15 non-AI corporate products across categories that could be rebuilt as AI-native versions.

## Requirements
1. Search across categories: dating, fitness, productivity, storage, finance, social, HR, legal, real estate, travel, food/restaurant, education
2. For each candidate product, note:
   - Product name and category
   - Current revenue / user base (approximate)
   - Core value proposition
   - Incumbent's current AI strategy (are they already doing this?)
3. Target 10-15 candidates minimum
4. Focus on products where AI fundamentally changes the experience (not just bolt-on features)
5. Prioritize products where the incumbent is slow to adopt AI

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] 10-15 candidate products identified
- [ ] Each has category, revenue estimate, and AI opportunity noted
- [ ] Output saved to working notes for next task
