# Deep Analysis of Candidates

## Type
RESEARCH

## Description
Analyze each candidate product on 5 scoring dimensions.

## Requirements
1. For each candidate from task 002, analyze:
   - **Ease of build** — can one person + AI agents ship an MVP? (1-10)
   - **Market size** — TAM and realistic capture rate (1-10)
   - **Incumbent vulnerability** — how slow is the incumbent to adopt AI? (1-10)
   - **Defensibility** — can the AI advantage be sustained? (1-10)
   - **Time-to-revenue** — how fast to first paying customer? (1-10)
2. Consider the thesis: incumbents are historically slow (Blockbuster/Netflix, Kodak/iPhone, taxi/Uber)
3. Weight "ease of build" and "time-to-revenue" higher — solo builder constraint
4. Factor in the 2-year window (2026-2028) before AI becomes table stakes

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] All candidates scored on 5 dimensions
- [ ] Scores are justified with brief rationale
- [ ] Clear differentiation between top and bottom candidates
