# Top 3 Deep Dives

## Type
RESEARCH

## Description
Deep dive research on the top 3 candidates from the scored matrix.

## Requirements
1. For each of the top 3 scored candidates, research and write up:
   - **Current product** — what it does, who uses it, pricing model
   - **AI-native reimagining** — how AI fundamentally changes the core experience (not bolt-on)
   - **Competitive landscape** — who else is building AI versions? How far along?
   - **Technical feasibility** — what APIs, models, and infrastructure needed?
   - **Go-to-market** — first target segment, acquisition channel, pricing
   - **Risks** — what could go wrong? Regulatory? Incumbent response?
2. Each deep dive should be 500-1000 words
3. Save to `projects/ai-clone-opportunity/top-3-deep-dives.md`

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `test -f projects/ai-clone-opportunity/top-3-deep-dives.md`
- [ ] Three distinct product deep dives included
- [ ] Each covers all 6 analysis areas
