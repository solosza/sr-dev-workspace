# Task 004: Products & Services + Marketing & Sales + Operations
**Type:** BUILD | **Gates:** OB-04
## Action
Write Products & Services, Marketing & Sales Strategy, Operations, and Organization & Management sections.
## Spec
**Products & Services:** describe the application in plain language — it reads the charting the therapist already did and the code about to be billed, checks it against Medicare's rules, and flags anything that needs a person to review before it's submitted; it keeps a clear record of why each claim was billed (audit protection). State the guarantee plainly: **a person always reviews and approves before any claim is billed; the application never submits a claim on its own.** **Marketing & Sales:** pilot-first at the cousin's facility; administrators champion, owner approves; proof = showing where the application agreed with the therapist's own judgment. **Operations:** how it fits the current workflow (adds one review step; therapist keeps charting as they do today). **Organization & Management:** the team (the on-the-ground RTs/validators + the builder) at a high level. NO engineering terms.
## Acceptance
Four sections written, plain language, guarantees stated in plain English, zero jargon.
