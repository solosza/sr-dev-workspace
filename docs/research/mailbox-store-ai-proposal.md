### The Problem

You spend 2-4 hours every day on the same cycle: scan a package, log it, snap a photo, shelve it, text the customer. Multiply that by 50-200 packages a day and your most valuable resource — your time — disappears into busywork. Meanwhile, customers call asking "is my package there yet?" and you're stuck answering the same question over and over.

### The Solution

Our Package Intake Agent automates the entire scan-to-notify pipeline. Scan a barcode, and the system instantly logs the package, captures a photo, and sends your customer an SMS or email — all in under 3 seconds. No manual data entry. No phone calls. No packages lost in the shuffle.

### What You Get

- **Instant Customer Notifications** — SMS and email sent automatically the moment you scan a package in. Customers stop calling to ask "is it there?"
- **Package Photo on File** — Every package is photographed at intake. No more disputes about condition or "I never received it."
- **Live Dashboard** — See every package in your store right now: what's waiting, what's been picked up, and what's been sitting too long.
- **Pickup Confirmation** — Scan the package out when the customer picks up. The loop closes automatically.
- **Daily Summary Report** — End-of-day snapshot: how many came in, how many went out, average wait time. Know your store's rhythm.

### How It Works

1. **Connect** — Plug in your existing barcode scanner and webcam. Install with one command. No new hardware needed.
2. **Scan** — Scan packages as they arrive, just like you do now. The system handles everything else — logging, photo, notification.
3. **Relax** — Customers get notified instantly. You check the dashboard when you want, not because someone is calling.
