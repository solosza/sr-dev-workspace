# Research Custom Emoji/Icon Market for Grid-Based Games

## Status
Open

## Priority
Medium — potential new revenue stream; D&D game engine already exists as a concrete use case to validate demand

## Summary
Grid-based games like the D&D engine at `D:\my_ai_projects\project_test_repos\dnd-game-engine-test` use emoji as visual tokens. Standard Unicode emoji are generic — no dungeon doors, torch sconces, goblin variants, spell effect tiles. This research answers two questions: (1) what free/licensed icon sets already exist for niche tabletop/dungeon games, and (2) is there a viable business in designing and selling custom emoji/iconography packs targeting game developers building grid-based engines?

## Requirements
- Inventory existing free and paid icon sets for tabletop/dungeon/fantasy grid games (itch.io, game-icons.net, OpenGameArt, commercial)
- Assess gaps: what categories of icons are missing or low-quality for D&D-style grid engines (terrain, conditions, spell effects, monster types)
- Identify sales channels for niche game icon packs (itch.io, Gumroad, direct licensing to game devs)
- Research pricing norms for indie game asset packs (icon sets, tile sets, emoji packs)
- Assess production pipeline: AI-generated art (Midjourney/DALL-E), human polish, export formats (SVG, PNG spritesheets, Apple/Google emoji-compatible format)
- Identify if emoji submission to Unicode or custom emoji platforms (Discord, Slack, Twitch) is a separate monetization angle
