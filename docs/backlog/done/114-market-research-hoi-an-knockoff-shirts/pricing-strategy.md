# Pricing Strategy — Landed Cost, Retail Pricing, Margin Targets

## Status
NEW

## Research Questions
- What is the realistic landed cost for a replica T-shirt and hoodie at 12, 36, and 72 unit batches from China?
- What do comparable replica resellers charge? What is the apparent markup over supplier cost?
- What platform fees apply at each sales channel (Depop 10%, eBay ~13%, etc.)?
- What retail price can the market bear for a "premium replica" vs a budget replica?
- If going private label: what's the cost delta for blank high-quality streetwear vs replica branded goods?
- What's the break-even unit count at target retail price?

## What to Produce
- Landed cost model: product cost + freight + duties + platform fees + packaging for T-shirt and hoodie
- Retail price recommendation per SKU at 3 margin targets (50%, 60%, 70% gross)
- Comparison table: replica branded vs private label — cost, margin, risk, scalability
- Break-even analysis: units needed to recover initial sourcing + setup investment

## Dependencies
- Logistics/fulfillment (freight and duty costs feed into landed cost)
- Market analysis (competitive pricing ceiling)
- Sourcing research (per-unit product cost)
