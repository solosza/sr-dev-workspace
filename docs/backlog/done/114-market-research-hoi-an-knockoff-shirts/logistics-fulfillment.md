# Logistics & Fulfillment — Import Duties, Freight, Small-Batch Options

## Status
NEW

## Research Questions
- What are the US HTS codes for knit T-shirts, woven shirts, and hoodies? What are the duty rates from China vs Vietnam?
- Does the de minimis threshold ($800 per shipment) apply to commercial replica imports, and is it a viable strategy for small batches?
- What are the air freight costs from Guangzhou and Da Nang for a 12-unit vs 36-unit apparel batch?
- Which freight forwarders or shipping agents specialize in small-batch China/Vietnam apparel imports?
- How do experienced replica resellers receive goods — direct to home, freight forwarder, reshipping service?
- What are the Customs seizure risks for apparel with visible branded logos at small quantities?

## What to Produce
- HTS code table: T-shirt, hoodie, cap — duty rate from China, duty rate from Vietnam (GSP status if applicable)
- Freight cost model: air vs sea, per-unit cost at 12 / 36 / 72 unit batches from both origins
- De minimis strategy assessment: viable or not for this use case
- 2-3 fulfillment options: self-fulfill from home, 3PL (ShipBob/Shipmonk), or dropship direct from supplier

## Dependencies
- Sourcing research (origin country affects duty rate)
- Legal/compliance (Customs seizure risk depends on branded vs unbranded)
