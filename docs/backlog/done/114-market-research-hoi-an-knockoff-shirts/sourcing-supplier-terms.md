## Research Questions
- What are the primary sourcing regions for replica streetwear? (Guangzhou Baiyun / Shahe market for China; Hội An / HCMC for Vietnam apparel?)
- How do China suppliers (DHgate, 1688, Alibaba) tier their quality? What are the grades (AAA, 1:1, etc.) and what do they mean in practice?
- Can Vietnam apparel suppliers (from the same Hội An/Da Nang network as leather goods) produce high-quality blank streetwear or do branded replicas?
- What does the sample ordering process look like — MOQ, sample cost, lead time?
- How do suppliers handle branding requests — can they apply Stussy/Supreme graphics, or is that negotiated separately?
- What payment methods do China replica suppliers accept? (Alipay, USDT, PayPal, wire?)
- What's the risk of supplier scams at small order volumes?
