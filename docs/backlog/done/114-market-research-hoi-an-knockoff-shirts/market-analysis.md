# Market Analysis — Replica Streetwear

## Status
NEW

## Research Questions
- Where is replica streetwear actually being sold to US buyers in 2025? (Depop, eBay, Discord servers, Telegram channels, DHgate storefronts, TikTok Shop?)
- Which brands have the highest replica demand by search volume / listing count? (Stussy, Supreme, Polo, Off-White, Bape, Essentials, Corteiz?)
- What price points are replicas selling for vs retail? What margin does the reseller appear to be capturing?
- Who are the top 5 visible resellers — how do they present inventory, handle DMs, what's their review profile?
- Is there a "safe" above-ground market (e.g., "vintage-inspired", "streetwear basics") that captures similar demand without trademark exposure?
- What product photos and listing formats drive sales in this category?

## What to Produce
- Platform map: where replicas are sold + risk level per platform
