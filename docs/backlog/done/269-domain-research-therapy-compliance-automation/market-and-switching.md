# Market & Switching Analysis

**Status:** NEW — research
**Location (research output):** projects/therapy-compliance-automation/market-and-switching.md

## What it needs to answer
- **The incumbent:** who currently does this for the facilities (the "current company"), what they charge, and that their charting/billing/compliance is manual with hand-corrected errors.
- **Why a facility/owner switches:** the value levers — fewer billing errors (denied-claim + clawback reduction), faster throughput, lower labor cost, audit-defensibility, compliance risk reduction. Quantify where possible (typical denial rates, error-correction cost) with cited sources.
- **The buyer & the sale:** administrators are the champion but OWNERS approve — what owners care about (revenue integrity, audit exposure, cost). This shapes the business plan.
- **Competitive landscape:** existing SNF/therapy compliance + billing vendors, EMR-native compliance modules, and whether an AI-native governed approach is differentiated or a feature others already ship.
- **Risks that could kill it:** Medicare billing-compliance LIABILITY (a wrong "eligible" = false-claim/OIG exposure — who is liable, us or the facility?), HIPAA/PHI handling and BAA requirements, EMR integration friction, CMS rule drift, and reimbursement/regulatory change risk.

## Output
A market viability assessment feeding the GO/NO-GO: is there a real, switchable buyer with budget, and do the risks stay manageable?
