# Research: Should isagawa.co Pivot to Loops and Agent Systems?

## Status
Open

## Priority
Medium — site messaging was just updated (backlog 142), but the market is moving fast toward "loops" and "agent systems" as primary framing. Worth researching before the next site revision.

## Summary
isagawa.co currently positions the kernel as "spec-driven loop engineering for AI agents" with emphasis on governed agent harnesses, domain-spec factory, and backlog pipeline. The question is whether the site should pivot to lead with "loops" and "agent systems" as the primary framing, leaning into the broader industry language around agentic loops, multi-agent orchestration, and agent system design. This research should determine if the current messaging is already well-positioned or if a pivot would better capture the audience searching for loop-based and agent-system tooling.

## Requirements
- Audit current isagawa.co messaging and identify what resonates vs what's unclear
- Research how competitors and the broader market frame "loops" and "agent systems"
- Identify search terms and positioning language people actually use when looking for this category of tooling
- Compare "spec-driven loop engineering" framing vs "agent systems" framing for clarity, discoverability, and differentiation
- Assess whether "loops" is too generic or if it captures the right audience
- Deliver a recommendation: pivot, stay, or hybrid approach with specific copy suggestions
