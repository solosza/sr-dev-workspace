# Curiosity Harness — Teaching Quality Questions in an AI World

## Status
Open

## Priority
Medium — strategic product direction; aligns with Isagawa's "agency over automation" philosophy

## Summary
Aravind Srinivas (Perplexity CEO, Joe Rogan episode) argues the future belongs to the curious — own your AI locally, kill algorithmic feeds, guard your agency, become interesting to talk to. The thesis: when AI can answer anything, the scarce skill is knowing what to ask. Research whether Isagawa should build a "curiosity harness" — a tool/framework that teaches people how to ask quality questions, think critically about AI output, and retain agency. Analyze alignment with Isagawa's existing kernel philosophy (self-building, safety-first, autonomy) and explore product opportunities.

## Source Material (Aravind Srinivas via Nav Toor @heynavtoor)

Key claims to evaluate:
1. **Own your AI, don't rent it** — local AI "refrigerator" boxes are coming; independence from big tech feeds
2. **Kill the algorithmic feed** — social media scroll is "brain rot that curbs curiosity"
3. **Work harder than comfortable** — intensity over balance for building great things
4. **Become interesting to talk to** — status shifts from employer to personal depth; "read wider, think stranger"
5. **Guard your agency** — retain curiosity and agency as AI proliferates; doom feeds kill human agency
