# Research Agentic AI System for Mailbox Stores

## Status
Open

## Priority
High — real business opportunity, owner has domain expertise

## Summary
Research and design an agentic AI system for mailbox store operations. The user owns a mailbox store and wants to build a system that can be sold to other mailbox store owners. Need to identify the operational pain points, repetitive workflows, and customer interaction patterns that AI agents can automate.

## Research Areas

### Operations
- Package tracking and notifications (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL integration)
- Mailbox rental management (new customers, renewals, expirations)
- Package intake logging (scan, photograph, notify customer)
- Customer communication (text/email notifications, reminders)
- Inventory management (shipping supplies, boxes, packing materials)
- Shipping label generation and rate comparison

### Customer Service
- Walk-in customer inquiries (package status, shipping quotes)
- Phone/text automation (package ready for pickup, mailbox renewal due)
- New customer onboarding (mailbox assignment, key management, ID verification)
- Notary scheduling and management

### Business Management
- Revenue tracking per service (mailbox rental, shipping, notary, printing)
- Customer retention (renewal reminders, usage patterns)
- Peak time prediction (holidays, tax season)
- Multi-location management (if scaling to franchise model)

### AI Agent Opportunities
- **Package intake agent** — scan barcode, photograph, auto-notify customer, log in system
- **Customer communication agent** — proactive notifications, pickup reminders, renewal alerts
- **Shipping advisor agent** — compare rates across carriers, recommend best option
- **Business analytics agent** — daily revenue summary, customer retention alerts, inventory reorder triggers

## Key Questions
- What POS/management software do mailbox stores typically use?
- What APIs are available for USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL integration?
- What's the typical tech stack in a mailbox store? (iPad at counter? Desktop?)
- What are the biggest time sinks for a mailbox store owner?
- What would another mailbox store owner pay for this?

## Output
- Research document with findings
- System architecture proposal
- MVP scope definition (smallest useful product)
- Pricing model for selling to other stores
