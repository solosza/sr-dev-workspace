# AI Clone Product Opportunity — Replace Non-AI Corporate Products

## Status
Open

## Priority
High — massive TAM, 2-year window before incumbents catch up; billion-dollar outcomes historically came from obvious-in-hindsight plays

## Summary
Research and identify the best non-AI corporate product to clone as an AI-native version. The thesis: take any successful non-AI consumer/prosumer product (Tinder, Dropbox, MyFitnessPal, etc.), rebuild it with AI at the core, and capture the market before the incumbent adapts. Most billion-dollar ideas were obvious in hindsight — the window is now while incumbents are slow to integrate AI deeply into their core product loops.

## Requirements
- Identify 10-15 candidate products across categories (dating, fitness, productivity, storage, finance, social)
- For each candidate, analyze: current revenue, user base, how AI would fundamentally change the product (not just bolt-on), incumbent's AI strategy (are they already doing this?), moat potential
- Score candidates on: ease of build (can one person + AI agents ship an MVP?), market size, incumbent vulnerability, defensibility of AI advantage, time-to-revenue
- Pick top 3 candidates with detailed rationale
- For the #1 pick: outline MVP feature set, tech stack, go-to-market, and estimated timeline to first revenue

## References
- Thesis: copy any non-AI corporate product and make an AI version
- Pattern: incumbents are slow — Blockbuster/Netflix, Kodak/iPhone, taxi/Uber
- Timing: 2-year window (2026-2028) before AI becomes table stakes for every product
- Build leverage: AI agents can ship product 10-100x faster than traditional dev teams
- **Backlog 034** — Website Cloner skill (build FIRST). Clone any website's UI via Playwright MCP in one prompt. Once research picks the #1 target, use 035 to rip the UI instantly, then rebuild the backend with AI.
