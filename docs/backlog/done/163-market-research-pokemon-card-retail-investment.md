# Research: Pokemon Card Retail Acquisition & Investment Analysis

## Status
Open

## Priority
Medium — personal investment research with potential agentic build opportunity

## Summary
Two-phase research into Pokemon card collecting as both a competitive retail game and an investment vehicle. Phase 1 explores how smart buyers use bots and automation to acquire Pokemon card boxes at retail price before other buyers — and whether we can build an agentic system to gain an edge. Phase 2 is an honest investment analysis: is Pokemon card investing actually a good ROI compared to stocks, real estate, and other tangible assets?

## Requirements
- Research current bot/automation landscape for retail Pokemon card acquisition
- Identify retailers, drop patterns, and automation tools smart buyers use
- Assess feasibility of building an agentic buyer (Playwright + monitoring)
- Analyze historical Pokemon card ROI data (sealed product, graded singles)
- Compare against S&P 500, real estate, gold, crypto, and other collectibles
- Provide honest risk assessment — not hype
