# Card Depth, Interactivity, and Tag Lists

## Status
NEW

## Card Hover Effects
- All evidence cards + attestation cards
- Hover: `translateY(-2px)`
- Border-color transition to `--accent` at `0.3` opacity
- Box-shadow: `--accent-glow` at `0.06` opacity
- Duration: `250ms ease`
- Subtle 1px top border: `--border-subtle` to `--accent` on hover

## Tag Lists Above Card Titles (Suero Pattern)

### Style
- Small caps
- Font: `--font-mono`
- Size: `--text-xs`
- Letter-spacing: `0.1em`
- Color: `--text-secondary`
- Gap above title: `0.5rem`
