## Research Questions
- What are the US import duty rates for leather bags and small leather goods? (HTS Chapter 42 — typical range 4-9%, but varies by item type)
- What does air freight cost for a 25-unit batch of bags from Da Nang to a US address? (DHL/FedEx international)
- At what volume does sea freight become cheaper than air freight?
- What US 3PLs are good for low-volume artisan goods (not requiring Amazon-scale MOQs)?
  - ShipBob, Shipmonk, Pirateship+home fulfillment, Fulfilled by Merchant
- What's the de minimis threshold impact? (shipments under $800 from Vietnam enter duty-free — is per-shipment splitting viable?)
- What customs documentation is needed for leather goods from Vietnam?
