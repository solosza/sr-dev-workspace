# Platform Decision — Etsy vs Shopify

## Status
NEW

## Research Questions
- What are the fee structures for Etsy (listing, transaction, payment processing) vs Shopify?
- Does Etsy's handmade policy allow reselling goods made by an overseas workshop you commissioned?
- What's the realistic time-to-first-sale on Etsy with no existing reviews vs cold Shopify traffic?
- Can you run both simultaneously without violating Etsy terms?
- What does a minimal Shopify setup cost (theme, apps) for a leather goods store?

## Recommendation Criteria
- Lowest barrier to first sale
- Brand control for premium positioning
- Ability to tell the supplier story (photos, origin, artisan narrative)

## What to Produce
- Platform comparison table (fees, time-to-market, brand control, traffic source)
- Recommended starting point with rationale
- If Etsy: listing setup checklist
- If Shopify: minimum viable store spec

## Dependencies
- Market analysis (what platform are competitors on?)
