# Message Research

## Status
NEW

## Purpose
Validate the "solo founder at team velocity, proven cryptographically" thesis before writing a single line of landing page HTML. Research should confirm the message is differentiated — not generic AI-developer noise — by comparing against how others in the market position similar work.

## Research Questions

### 1. How do AI engineers and solo founders currently position themselves?
- Search for portfolio sites of "AI engineers," "agentic systems engineers," "forward deployed engineers"
- What do they lead with? Skills lists? Projects? Demos?
- Is cryptographic provenance / attestation present anywhere?
- Does anyone lead with output-per-person velocity as the primary claim?

### 2. What does the market actually want right now?
- Search recent FDE / Applied AI Engineer job postings (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Scale AI, Palantir)
- What do the JDs emphasize beyond technical skills? Autonomy? Velocity? Evidence of shipping?
- Does "governance" / "provenance" / "observability" appear as a differentiator?

### 3. How do funded teams position similar capabilities?
- Search Factory.ai, Cognition (Devin), Poolside, Magic.dev — how do they frame autonomous coding?
- What claims do they make that can't be made by a solo founder?
- What claims can ONLY be made by a solo founder (i.e., output-per-person at this scale)?

### 4. Is "cryptographic proof of autonomous work" already a category?
- Search "AI attestation portfolio," "agent provenance," "Sigstore AI pipeline"
- Is anyone else doing this for a portfolio? For a product?
- Does it require explanation or does it land immediately?

### 5. What resonates in technical pitches right now?
- Search recent HN "Show HN" posts for AI agent tools, agentic pipelines, solo founder AI projects
- What gets upvoted? What gets ignored?
- Do demos beat credentials? Do numbers beat narrative?

## Output
A research report at `projects/portfolio-message-research/research-report.md` covering:
1. **Market landscape:** How others position (summary of findings)
2. **Gaps:** What nobody is saying that we could own
3. **Validation verdict:** Does the solo-founder-velocity thesis hold up? Is it differentiated?
4. **Refined message:** Adjusted copy if research suggests tighter framing
5. **Evidence to surface:** Which specific numbers/claims will land hardest based on what the market responds to

## Deliverable Gate
The landing page build (page-spec.md) does NOT start until this report exists and the message is confirmed or refined. The task builder should sequence these as Phase 1 → Phase 2.
