# Research Business Credit Stacking — Self-Funded Startup Runway

## Status
Open

## Priority
Medium — funding strategy that could eliminate need for outside investors

## Summary
Research the business credit stacking strategy described by @hxxntrr: take $250K in business credit from banks, pay yourself a $7K/month salary for 3 years while building a business. This is how funded startups operate — the founder draws a salary from raised capital. Validate legality, realistic credit limits for new LLCs, interest rates, and risk profile.

## Source
@hxxntrr (May 11, 2026): "You can take a quarter million dollars from American banks and pay yourself a $7,000/month salary for 3 years while you build a business from scratch and the banks can't do a thing about it because that's a legitimate business expense. This is how funded startups work."

## Requirements
- Research business credit cards vs. business lines of credit vs. SBA microloans
- Validate realistic credit limits for a new LLC (is $250K achievable? timeline?)
- Calculate true cost: interest rates, minimum payments, credit score impact
- Research the "credit stacking" method — multiple cards/lines across banks
- Identify risks: personal guarantee exposure, default consequences
- Compare to alternatives: SBA loans, ROBS (401k rollover), bootstrapping
- Assess applicability: could this fund the govcon LLC or RT automation MVP?
