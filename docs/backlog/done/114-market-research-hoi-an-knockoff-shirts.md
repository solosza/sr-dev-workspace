# Research: Replica Streetwear — US Resale Business (Stussy, Polo, Supreme, etc.)

## Status
Open

## Priority
High — sourcing infrastructure from 113 leather goods research applies directly; replica streetwear is higher velocity, lower AOV, larger addressable market

## Summary
Research and plan a resale business importing high-quality replica streetwear (Stussy, Ralph Lauren Polo, Supreme, Off-White, Bape, etc.) sourced from Vietnam or China. Unlike leather goods, this category carries trademark risk — research must address legal/compliance posture, platform policies, and whether a private-label pivot is the smarter play. Deliverable is an actionable sourcing + sales plan with a clear risk assessment.

## Context

- Target brands to replicate or reference: Stussy, Ralph Lauren Polo, Supreme, Off-White, Bape, Essentials
- Category: T-shirts, hoodies, zip-ups, caps — high-turnover streetwear
- Key question: sell as replicas (higher risk, higher demand) vs private label / inspired-by (lower risk, needs brand building)
- Vietnam sourcing already explored via leather goods trip — same supplier network may extend to apparel
- China (Guangzhou/1688) is primary global source for replica streetwear; need quality tier research

## Requirements

- Identify where replica streetwear is actually being sold in the US (Depop, eBay, Discord, Telegram, DHgate storefronts, etc.)
- Map the 3-5 most cloned brands by demand volume — which logos/graphics move fastest
- Define the risk spectrum: replica vs "inspired by" vs private label — legal exposure at each tier
- Identify 3-5 China/Vietnam suppliers with verifiable quality (photos, sample ordering process)
- Determine apparel import duties (US HTS codes for knit tops, woven shirts, hoodies)
- Output a landed cost model for a T-shirt and hoodie at 12, 36, and 72 unit batches
- Recommend the lowest-risk, highest-margin path: replica underground vs private label brand
