# Investment Analysis — Pokemon Cards vs Traditional Assets

## Status
NEW

## Purpose
Honest assessment of Pokemon card investing as a return vehicle. Compare historical and projected ROI against traditional investments. No hype — data-driven analysis with risk assessment.

## Research Questions

### Pokemon Card ROI Data
- What's the historical appreciation of sealed Pokemon product? (booster boxes, ETBs by set)
- Which sets/eras have appreciated most? (vintage WOTC, modern chase sets, Japanese exclusives)
- What's the ROI on graded singles? (PSA 10 Charizards, trophy cards, modern alt arts)
- What's the typical holding period for meaningful returns?
- What percentage of product actually appreciates vs. depreciates?

### Cost Structure & Hidden Costs
- Storage costs (climate controlled for sealed product)
- Grading costs (PSA/BGS fees, turnaround times, submission risk)
- Insurance and security
- Transaction fees (eBay 13%, TCGPlayer fees, auction house commissions)
- Opportunity cost of capital tied up in cardboard

### Comparison Against Traditional Investments
- S&P 500 average annual return (~10% nominal, ~7% real)
- Real estate (appreciation + rental income)
- Gold / precious metals
- Crypto (BTC, ETH — high volatility comparison)
- Other collectibles (sports cards, comic books, vintage toys, wine)
- Bonds / fixed income

### Risk Assessment
- Market saturation (Pokemon Company print runs increasing)
- Condition sensitivity (sealed product storage risk)
- Liquidity risk (can you actually sell at "market price"?)
- Counterfeiting risk
- Fad/bubble risk — is the current market sustainable?
- Tax implications (collectibles taxed at 28% capital gains vs 15-20% for stocks)

### Investment Outlook
- Short-term (1-3 years)
- Medium-term (5-10 years)
- Long-term (10+ years)
- What does the Pokemon brand trajectory look like?
- Print run trends and their impact on future scarcity

## Dependencies
- Historical price data from eBay sold listings, PSA cert verification, TCGPlayer
- Market analysis from Pokemon investing communities
- Financial comparison data from standard market indices
