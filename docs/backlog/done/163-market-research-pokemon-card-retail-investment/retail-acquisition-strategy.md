# Retail Acquisition Strategy — Pokemon Cards

## Status
NEW

## Purpose
Research how smart buyers use bots and automation to acquire Pokemon card sealed product (booster boxes, ETBs, special sets) at retail price before other buyers. Assess whether an agentic system could provide a competitive edge.

## Research Questions

### Current Landscape
- What retailers sell Pokemon cards online at MSRP? (Pokemon Center, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, GameStop, Costco)
- What are typical restock/drop patterns and schedules?
- How quickly do high-demand sets sell out at retail?
- What's the markup difference between retail and secondary market?

### Bot & Automation Ecosystem
- What tools/bots do "smart buyers" currently use? (browser extensions, Discord monitors, auto-checkout bots)
- How do stock monitoring services work? (NowInStock, BrickSeek, Discord restock alerts)
- What anti-bot measures do retailers deploy? (CAPTCHA, queue systems, purchase limits)
- What's the legal/ethical landscape of using bots for retail purchases?

### Agentic Buying Feasibility
- Could a Playwright-based agent monitor retailer sites for restocks?
- Could it auto-add-to-cart and checkout faster than manual buyers?
- What are the technical barriers? (CAPTCHA solving, account bans, shipping limits)
- Is this worth building vs. just using existing Discord alert services?
- Cost-benefit: development time vs. expected savings per box

## Dependencies
- Web research (retailer sites, Reddit communities, Discord groups)
- No account creation or actual purchases needed
