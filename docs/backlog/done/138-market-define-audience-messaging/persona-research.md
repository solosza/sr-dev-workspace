## Personas to define

### 1. AI Infrastructure / Agent Orchestration Lead
- Role: Infrastructure engineer, platform architect, tech lead at AI company
- Pain points: Managing multi-agent complexity, enforcement consistency, testing coverage at scale
- Priorities: Governance, safety, scalability, testability
- Success metric: Reduced infrastructure bugs, faster agent development cycles, audit compliance
- Language preference: Technical, architecture-focused, mentions enforcement patterns

### 2. Compliance / Regulatory Automation Specialist
- Role: Automation engineer in regulated industries (healthcare, finance, legal)
- Pain points: Audit trail requirements, testing rigor, regulatory change tracking
- Priorities: Safety verification, audit compliance, regulatory adaptability
- Success metric: Passed audits, reduced compliance risk, faster rule updates
- Language preference: Business-focused, outcomes-oriented, mentions governance and trust

### 3. Early-Stage Founder (AI Platform / Tool Builder)
- Role: Founder/CTO building AI-native product or internal tools
- Pain points: Limited engineering resources, need for speed, safety concerns
- Priorities: Extensibility, quick iteration, built-in safety guarantees
- Success metric: Faster feature releases, fewer safety incidents, ability to scale team
- Language preference: Forward-looking, entrepreneurial, mentions innovation and speed
