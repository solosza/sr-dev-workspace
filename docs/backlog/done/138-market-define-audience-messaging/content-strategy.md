## Content mapping

### Where each audience lands
- **Infrastructure teams:** GitHub repo, technical docs, architecture diagrams
- **Compliance specialists:** Website features/benefits section, case studies, regulatory examples
- **Founders:** Website homepage, quickstart guide, extensibility showcase

### Content pieces to create per audience

#### For Infrastructure Teams
- Architecture diagrams explaining enforcement loop
- Technical documentation on hook patterns
- Example: multi-agent governance in production
- Community examples (GitHub discussions)

#### For Compliance Teams
- Regulatory use case page
- Audit trail documentation
- Example: compliance verification at scale
- Case studies from regulated industries

#### For Founders
- Quickstart guide emphasizing speed
- "Build a domain spec in 5 minutes" tutorial
- Extensibility examples
- Growth story: how the platform scales with your team

## Coordination points
- Homepage hero messaging ties to all three audiences
- Messaging variants inform content tone and focus
- Audience-specific landing pages or sections
- Navigation hierarchy reflects audience priorities
