## Summary
Research and plan a small import business selling handmade leather goods from a Hội An/Da Nang family workshop to US customers. The supplier relationship is already established — $800 spent in-shop, custom orders fulfilled same/next day via Grab, contact number in hand. Research covers market viability, supplier formalization, platform selection, import logistics, and pricing strategy. Deliverable is an actionable go-to-market plan ready to execute.

## Context (from direct experience)
- Supplier: family workshop, Da Nang/Hội An area, already have WhatsApp number
- Quality: exceptional, handmade leather — duffel bags ~$80 USD (2M dong), shoulder bag custom-made in 1 day for ~$40 USD (1M dong)
- Fulfillment speed: same-day or next-day via Grab delivery to Airbnb
- Relationship: $800 spent in one visit, personal rapport established
- Items purchased: 10-15 bags, mix of styles

## Requirements
- Identify 3-5 comparable sellers on Etsy — pricing, positioning, review volume, what's selling
- Define what a "spec sheet" looks like for leather goods (dimensions, hardware, stitching, color options)
- Determine realistic per-unit import cost including duties + freight at 10, 25, 50 unit batches
- Identify 2-3 US 3PL options suitable for small leather goods (ShipBob, Shipmonk, etc.)
- Recommend platform: Etsy first (lower barrier, built-in audience for handmade) vs Shopify (brand control)
- Output a simple landed cost → retail price model for 3 SKUs (shoulder bag, duffel, tote)
