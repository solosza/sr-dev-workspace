# Research: ADU Laws — Las Vegas (Clark County) & Honolulu County

## Status
Open

## Priority
Medium — owner has properties in both jurisdictions; actionable "how to add an ADU" breakdown wanted

## Summary
Full, current breakdown of Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) rules for the two counties where the owner holds property: the Las Vegas / Clark County, NV area (owner believes there is a recent casita-specific ADU ordinance) and the City & County of Honolulu, HI. Deliverable is a decision-grade research report per county: what's allowed, size/setback/parking rules, owner-occupancy and permit process, cost/timeline, and the exact steps to add an ADU to an existing single-family lot.

## Requirements
- **Jurisdiction precision:** Las Vegas is layered — City of Las Vegas, City of Henderson, City of North Las Vegas, and unincorporated Clark County each have their own zoning code. Identify which body governs, and explicitly run down the **recent Clark County / Las Vegas "casita" ADU ordinance** the owner referenced (find the ordinance number, effective date, and what it changed). Honolulu ADUs are governed by the City & County of Honolulu ADU ordinance (Bill 20 / ROH Chapter 21 lineage) — confirm current rules and any 2024–2026 amendments.
- **Per county, cover:** permitted zones / lot-size minimums; max ADU size (sq ft or % of primary); attached vs. detached vs. internal/garage-conversion rules; setbacks, height, lot coverage; **parking requirements** (often the binding constraint); **owner-occupancy requirement** (yes/no, which unit); short-term-rental restrictions; utility/sewer connection rules; number of ADUs allowed per lot.
- **Process & cost per county:** permit path (planning review, building permit, inspections), typical fees, impact/sewer connection fees, realistic timeline, and whether ministerial/by-right vs. discretionary (variance/CUP) approval.
- **Actionable "add an ADU" checklist** per county: ordered steps from confirming eligibility → design → permit submittal → build → final inspection/certificate of occupancy.
- **Source discipline:** cite official sources (county/city municipal code sections, planning-department ADU pages, the actual ordinance/bill text) with dates. Flag anything that is proposed-but-not-yet-effective vs. currently in force. Note that laws change — timestamp the report and name the code sections so it can be re-verified.
- **Gaps/decisions surfaced:** call out anything that depends on the specific parcel (zone, lot size, existing coverage) that the owner must supply to get a definitive answer.
