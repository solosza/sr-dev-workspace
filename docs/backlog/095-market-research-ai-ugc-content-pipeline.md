# Research AI UGC Content Pipeline — Seedance + Brand Contracts

## Status
Open

## Priority
Low — interesting revenue model but further from core competencies

## Summary
Research the AI-generated UGC (user-generated content) pipeline demonstrated by @twoclipping: a Japan travel vlog made 100% with AI (Seedance) in 8 minutes for $2 in compute. No creator, no flights, no shoot day. The thesis is that travel brands, luggage companies, travel insurance, tourism boards, and hotel chains will pay for AI-generated content that replaces traditional creator partnerships. Validate whether brands are actually buying this and what the revenue model looks like.

## Source
@twoclipping (May 11, 2026): "this japan vlog is 100% ai. made with seedance in 8 minutes. $2 in compute. no creator. no flights. no shoot day. every ai ugc operator is making the same product demo ads. travel brands. luggage. travel insurance. tourism boards. hotel chains. 90% of the dtc catalog is wide open."

## Requirements
- Research Seedance capabilities and current pricing
- Validate whether brands are actually purchasing AI-generated UGC at scale
- Identify the go-to-market: how do AI UGC creators find and close brand deals?
- Assess quality bar: can AI UGC pass platform authenticity checks (TikTok, Instagram)?
- Research competitive landscape: how many people are already doing this?
- Calculate unit economics: $2 compute per video vs. brand contract pricing
- Determine if this is a one-person operation or needs a team
